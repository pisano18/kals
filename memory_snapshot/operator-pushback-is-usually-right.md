When Joe questions a conclusion, treat it as a signal I stopped too early and
**go re-derive it**. On 2026-09-08 several of his challenges each changed the
answer materially: the hedge idea he proposed would have destroyed 26.5% of all
profit at the trigger I'd have picked, and his "why aren't you looking there?"
caught me hunting for a warning signal after I had already found the broken
measurement itself.

**BUT HE EXPLICITLY DOES NOT WANT DEFERENCE.** His words, 2026-09-09:
*"that doesn't mean blindly believe what I say I hate that. Even I need to be
argued with and researched and validated."*

**Why:** I converge on the first plausible explanation and look for
confirmation; he notices the skipped step. That makes his challenges worth
re-deriving. It does NOT make his proposals right — his own hedge idea was
measured and rejected at the trigger he suggested, and he wanted that said
plainly rather than softened.

**How to apply:**
- Challenged? Re-measure, don't defend. Report refutations of my own proposals
  as prominently as the proposals.
- Proposed something? Test it against data and say so when it fails. Agreeing
  without checking is the failure mode he named.
- He skims long messages. Put conclusions and open questions somewhere short
  and fixed — `results/SKIM.md` — not buried in prose. See
  [[handoff-is-the-only-continuity]].
- He prefers "this is unproven" over a confident number later withdrawn.
