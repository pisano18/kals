When Joe is mid-conversation with someone else — a support live chat, a call, anyone waiting on him — answer in one line FIRST, then do the analysis. On 2026-09-16 he asked "Should I send this" during a Crypto.com support chat; I spent several minutes on a recorder fix before replying and the agent had already moved on. His words: *"it is a LIVE CHAT you can't take 5 minutes to respond to that they already replied"*.

**Why:** his clock is the other party's patience, not mine. A perfect answer after the window closes is worth nothing, and background work is almost never more urgent than a person waiting.

**How to apply:** when a message contains a draft to approve, a yes/no, or anything with someone else waiting, respond in the same turn with the shortest useful answer and no tool calls. Queue the measurement work for after. Related: [[operator-pushback-is-usually-right]].
