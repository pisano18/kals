## L1 — rank coins on who will SELL to us, not on who has lost

**F-P11** no coin is reliably safer (split-half rank correlation +0.35 on 9
coins; a 4.12 pp spread appears 53% of the time by chance).
**F-M07** where nobody offered, the model misses its own claim by 0.028 pp;
where somebody did, by 0.725 pp — 26x.

F-P11 died because it ranked coins on a RARE event: 18 losses over 9 coins is
noise, and a null there cannot be told from no power. F-M07 names what really
separates a dangerous observation — somebody will sell us the side the model
likes. **Per coin, that willingness is a high-n rate, not a rare event.** So:
on the existing 46,153-observation model-certain grid, compute per coin (a) the
share with a takeable ask on the model's side, standardised onto one common
(stated-confidence x tau) mix (the `RESULTS_select.md` §2b estimator, which
showed the crude 26x is partly occupation-time selection), split-half for
stability; and (b) the realised z of the settle against the model's own
distribution, defined on EVERY observation, since the offered arm holds only 8
failures. Then test whether high-(a) coins carry more tail inflation
(`Var(z) > 1`). If (a) is stable and predicts (b), it is a pre-trade,
sigma-free coin dial — deployed as PRIORITY in the contract budget, never
exclusion (`PREREG_coinrank.md` §3: dropping coins is what cost money).

**Needs:** the certain grid and standardiser already built for the select
stage, plus `fulltape/markets.json`. No book rebuild, no replay.

**Artefact if:** (a) measures book PRESENCE rather than willingness — condition
on an ask existing on at least one side (F-O04 makes that nearly free). Without
the (confidence x tau) standardisation, (a) is a relabelling of the coin's
sigma-to-strike scale (F-M09's trap), since nobody sells a deep-OTM side.
`Var(z)` inflation is also produced by a frozen sigma EWMA and by incomplete
60-print windows (F-I05), so print per-coin index completeness beside it. **The
limiting n is still 9-12 coins per half, exactly as in F-P11** — print each
coin's (a) with a close-time-clustered interval and state the MDE on the
split-half rank correlation BEFORE looking at it, or a null repeats F-P11's
unresolvable result. All tape, all about what the index did and what the model
computed: never our loss rate.

**Cost:** ~half a day plus its self-test. Read-only.
