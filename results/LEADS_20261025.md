## L1 — quote age vs the MODEL's miss rate, from the book channel

**Facts collided.** F-I02 (we record `orderbook_delta` and `orderbook_snapshot`,
not only `trade`) x F-R06 (rebuilding the "someone sold it to us" population
from the TRADE tape has no power: +1.27 pp [-1.62, +4.57], MDE 4.05).

**The measurement.** F-R06 had to *infer* an offer's history from trades; the
book channel states the best ask's age outright. On an exogenous grid of
times-to-close (not trade arrivals), bucket every candidate by that age
(<0.25 s, 0.25-1 s, 1-5 s, 5-30 s, 30 s+) and compute **how often the model's
claimed-safe side actually settled wrong** in each bucket, inside tau bands.
That is a claim about the model and the index, which the tape is valid for; it
is **not** our loss rate and must never be quoted as one. It locates the age
knee F-O06 asserted post-hoc (0 failures in 302 at 30 s+). Only live fills
price a gate in dollars.

**Data.** The two book channels, the 1/sec index, `fulltape/markets.json`,
`pinlevels_rows.jsonl` (16,683 candidate instants, F-I09).

**Power, stated first.** Clustered by close time at rho 0.62-0.69 the design
effect is 31-46x (F-R02), so 16,683 candidates are worth ~350-550 independent
observations. At a 2.90% base miss rate (F-M04) that calls a ~2.5x lift and
nothing smaller (cf. F-M11's 2.50x). A 1.5x effect is
invisible — no power, not no effect.

**Artefacts that would fake it.**
1. **Tau.** Late in a window everything is re-quoted, and late is where the
   model is worst (F-M05), so age would be reading tau. Hence the tau bands.
2. **Tick zone.** The tick is 0.1c above 90c (F-X01), mean entry 95.6c (F-P05);
   fine-tick levels churn more, so "young" may just mean "above 90c" — which
   already sorts by confidence.
3. **Gaps age everything at 0:** the first snapshot after a restart, gzip
   truncation (F-I05), a silent field rename (F-I06) — worst in busy hours.
   Needs a per-hour parse check and a healthy-feed null for the gap guard.
4. **Population.** These buckets are "an ask existed", 31x from our fills
   (F-R05). A ranking says where to look, never that a gate pays.

**Read first:** IDEA_LEDGER P01 (`--toxic-fresh`), F09, W16 ("tape population
only"). If the knee is fixed there, this is dead.

**Cost.** One stage. Book loads are 2-3 GB each: cap concurrency, prefer cached
cells. Hours cold, minutes after.
