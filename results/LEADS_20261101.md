## L1. Is the post-loss loss cluster just exposure? (F-C08 x F-P08)

- **F-C08** — mean `abs(fair - 50c)` at the open is 4.75c; 40% of windows open outside 45-55c.
- **F-P08** — the close following a loss loses 9.4% (3 of 32) vs a 3.2% base.

A loss means the index moved hard, and `strike(N+1) == settle(N)`, so the next window opens further from 50c and throws more extreme candidates. Measure CONTRACTS and CANDIDATE COUNT at the close after each of our 60 losses against the all-closes base — exposure, not outcome. If exposure is up ~3x, F-P08 needs no accuracy story and the fix is a next-close size cap, not a cooldown.

Data: the 1/sec index for `abs(settle(N) - strike(N))`; `pinlevels_rows.jsonl` for candidate counts; our fill logs and the ledger for contracts and loss timestamps.

Artefact: three events. 32 closes carry ~1.0 expected losses at the 3.2% base, so F-P08's headline is not significant — and that fact line records its predecessor ("0 of 18") falsified by this same arithmetic. F-M10 already nulls the accuracy channel at 18,178 coin-closes (0.86x, [0.55, 1.36]). Cluster by close time. **MDE first:** contracts-per-close is continuous, 60 post-loss closes against ~1,800, so a 2x exposure shift is detectable; the loss RATE is not, at any n we have.

Cost: hours; index pass plus two log joins, no replay.

## L2. The bank at which size stops converting (F-B04 x F-O04)

- **F-B04** — size follows the bank, so consecutive worst-case losses shrink 33%, 22%, 15%.
- **F-O04** — 83.3% of the time there is NO ask at any price on the side the model wants.

Measure obtainable contracts at or below our ceiling on the model's side at each decision instant, summed across levels, over ALL candidates — not just the 16.7% with supply. Output: the bank at which `size = bank/5.88` outruns typical supply, and whether the post-loss shrink forgoes any fills.

Data: `orderbook_snapshot`/`delta` at the 16,683 candidate instants.

Artefact: posted depth is an UPPER BOUND — others take the whole level 68.6% of the time within 1 s. A silent book-reconstruction failure reads as a thin market (F-I06), so the depth guard needs its own null on a healthy feed. Design effect is 31-46x (F-R02): 16,683 candidates are worth a few hundred observations.

Cost: one book pass; the guard's null is the real work.
