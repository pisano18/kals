**Built but OFF in the live command line:**
- `--jump-widen`: after a jump, treat prices as twice as jumpy for buys and hedges.
- `--hedge-price`: hedge only if the market price also fell.
- `--hedge-normal`: hedge only if the other side passes the normal buy checks.
- `--hedge-jump`: hedge on a jump without waiting for confidence to fall.
- `--hedge-plant`: a 1-contract hedge test.
- `--skip-band`: refuse a chosen price band.
- `--band-mult`: bigger bets in a price band (1.5× at 90–94¢ ran 9/18–9/20).
- `--sigma-stress`: treat prices as more jumpy everywhere.
- `--rebuy-hedged`: after a hedge, buy more of the hedged side using the whole budget.
- `--attempts-on-send`: count only sent orders toward the try limits.
- `--edge-floor`: raise the minimum profit above 0.3¢.
- `--fresh-min-age-ms`: refuse young offers; the seller gate is the version that runs live.
- `--series` for the ETH/SOL/XRP/DOGE/BNB/HYPE hourly markets, and the 7 far-strike flags (buy far hourly strikes up to 99¢).

Some settings were live in between but weren't running at 9/14, 9/28 or now: the 97.8¢ main cap (9/27–9/28), the 95¢ floor on the 31–45 s buy (9/23–9/28), a full bet at 31–45 s (9/17–9/22) and the 97.5¢ cap on the 31–45 s buy (9/20).

**Not changed since 9/14:** 99.5% confidence to buy, 0.3¢ minimum profit, main window 3–30 s, 98¢ cap, 2 bets per quarter-hour (before the extra bet), the stop after 2 losing bets, and the stop at a 20% drop from the highest bank.
