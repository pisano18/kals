# LEADS 2026-09-25

Two proposals. **Nothing here is measured** — this box holds no data.

## L1 — F-M02 x F-O04: is the missing ask arithmetic, not information?

F-M02: `sd/sigma` collapses far faster than `sqrt(tau)`. F-O04: 83.3% of the
time there is no ask on the side the model wants, and 100% of those carry an ask
on the LOSING side.

**Measure.** On the select-stage population, tabulate that ask's price `q` by
tau. A maker selling it at `q` values our side at `>= 1-q`. The
tick above 90c is 0.1c (F-X01), so 99.9c is the top quotable price: if
`q <= 0.1c` they *cannot* quote our side above their own value, and the absence
is the grid ceiling, not a refusal. F-M02 is why `q` should pin to the floor
late. If it is the grid, F-O04 stops reading as an informed counterparty
refusing us — which also colours F-M07.

**Artefact.** Our own filters. The openwindow run dropped 215,711 quotes
"out-of-range", and a range filter deletes grid-extreme prices first — exactly
our side. Count dropped rows sitting on the model's side; book gaps (F-I05,
F-I06) do the same. `nothing` unless that is ~zero.

**Cost / MDE.** Book plus index at candidate seconds, population already built;
`load_quotes` holds 2-3 GB, one at a time. Moderate. +/-4pp on ~700 clusters.

## L2 — F-C07 x F-I09: score the model by how doubtful its vol estimate is

F-C07: fair's sensitivity to sigma is exactly zero at 50c. F-I09: 16,683
candidate rows on disk, 2,699 already refused.

**Measure.** Compute each row's fair twice — with the bot's own sigma, and with
a strictly backward-looking pooled sigma for that coin — and take the gap **in
fair units, not sigma units**. F-C07 makes that the right currency: a big sigma
disagreement near 50c is worth nothing, and fair units price it so. It is the
coin-comparable coordinate F-M09 says raw sigma lacks. Bucket the model's error
rate on it; see if the dump guard already catches the top bucket.

**Artefact.** The gap grows mechanically as sigma shrinks, so a hit may be
F-M08's low-sigma fifth relabelled. Bucket *within* sigma quintiles; if it stops
sorting, it is nothing. The pooled sigma must use nothing after the decision
second (F-O05).

**Cost / MDE.** `pinlevels_rows.jsonl` plus the index, no book load — cheap.
~480 effective observations after the 31-46x design effect (F-R02), ~14 errors
at the 2.90% base; five buckets gives ~5pp each — detectable only above ~8%.
Below that this is **no power**, not no effect.
