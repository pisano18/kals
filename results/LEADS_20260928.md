## 1. F-O03 x F-O07 — the one counterparty metric the era split never ran

- **F-O03** — they consume the whole level 68.6% of the time.
- **F-O07** — early vs late tape: contest 88.6% -> 86.8%, first take 66 -> 70 ms,
  depth UP. "Only the model's accuracy changed."

**Measure.** Split `exhausted` (competing contracts >= the depth we measured)
on close TIME, early tape vs late, over the same cached `contest_map` F-O07 used
— it already carries `exhausted`, `depth` and `close` per row, so this is a
re-score, not a tape walk. F-O07 rests on three metrics and this is the fourth;
the claim it supports — that what rotted is our model, not the other side — is
what pointed two weeks of work at the model instead of at a gate. Report the
fraction per era **at matched resting depth**, clustered by close.

**Why it is not obvious.** F-O07 measured depth going UP, and a deeper level is
mechanically harder to consume whole, so the null here predicts consumption
FALLING. A flat or rising number is the interesting result, and it would mean
the counterparty got more aggressive in the one dimension nobody looked at.

**Artefact if.** (a) **Depth mix** — consumption is a ratio with depth in the
denominator, so any unmatched depth shift produces the whole effect; match on
resting size or the number is depth wearing a hat. (b) **Tau and price mix** —
thin late-window books differ from mid-window ones; compare within tau x price
cells. (c) **Torn hours** (F-I05/F-I06) are not random on the clock; print
surviving closes per era. (d) Strictly unconditional — no forward window, or
F-O05 leaks the outcome (3.18 pp at 250 ms, 88.55 pp at 60 s).

**Legal under rule 5.** This is what the MARKET did on the tape's population.
It is never our loss rate and must not be quoted as one.

**MDE first.** ~2,140 closes at ~1.22 effective each (F-R01) is ~1,750; on a
50/50 era split at p ~ 0.69 that is se ~ 2.2 pp, so about **6 pp** is findable
and anything smaller is **no power, not no effect**. Compute rho for the
`exhausted` indicator itself — importing F-R02's bad-rate design effect would
manufacture no-power (the mistake LEADS_20260913 #2 flagged).

**Cost.** Minutes. The cache is built; nothing reloads the tape.
