"Find a way or a version that will scale all the way up, or something that makes a little money but has tons of markets so it can scale. Even if it has to be an entirely new thing, that's fine, as long as it's something that consistently compounds and doesn't cap out even in the ten thousands."

Success = a strategy (new, or a variation of a current bot) with ALL of:
1. Positive expected return per day that is measured from recorded data, not assumed, with the fill model charged pessimistically (queue ahead, latency, fees).
2. Capacity: still earning at least 0.5% a day with a $10,000 bank and at least 0.25% a day with a $50,000 bank, shown as a capacity curve ($/day against bank size) from the tape, not extrapolated.
3. A loss model with a bounded worst case: the most that can be lost in one close/day as a share of the bank, and how often losses are expected, from data.
4. A path to run it: paper first (the clean-room style used for the ladder: a paper bot that logs its own queue-ahead and pessimistic fills), then live with the operator's rails.
If no single strategy meets all four, the answer is the best PORTFOLIO of uncorrelated strategies that together do, with the allocation across them.
