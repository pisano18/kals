## 1. F-I10 x F-M03 — is the deterioration a hole in the tape?

- **F-I10** — a killed recorder costs up to 5 minutes of tape.
- **F-M03** — the model claims it will be wrong 0.15% of the time.

**Measurement.** By F-I05 the loss is worse than 5 minutes — a mid-hour restart
leaves two gzip members and the standard reader recovers ZERO lines from the
hour. Count per close how many of the 900 window-second and 60
settlement-second `cfbenchmarks_value` prints exist on disk (via `gzsalvage`)
and plot coverage against close time. The unexplained doubling of the candidate
bad rate (1.87% -> 4.61%) then gets a rival explanation: a thinner late tape
rebuilds `locked_sum` from fewer prints.

**Data.** Index channel only, no replay. Minutes to run.

**MDE.** Coverage-vs-time is descriptive; flat coverage kills this for free.
The bad-rate split is underpowered — ~1,672 closes at a 2.9% base, 10%
incomplete, MDE **~+3.4 pp** against an observed 2.7 pp.

**Artefact.** The standard reader returns zero on a two-member file and would
manufacture this pattern — prove 3,600 prints on a healthy hour first.
Volatility drives file size, restarts AND the bad rate; hold it fixed.

## 2. F-P10 x F-X05 — maker fill rate without the queue simulator

- **F-P10** — making at the touch earns +0.48c/fill; never turned into dollars
  because queue position is unmeasured.
- **F-X05** — sweeps print per level, median 8 legs.

**Measurement.** A take that clears a level ENTIRELY fills every resting order
there, queue position irrelevant. Group same-instant trades; per touch take,
record whether it cleared the level. The share of touch volume from takes that
would still clear a level holding S more contracts **lower-bounds a resting
quote's fill rate with no simulator** — times takes/day times 0.48c, a floor on
$/day.

**Data.** Trade channel plus `flow.py`'s rebuilt book for resting size — seconds
on a warm `flow` cache, ~100 minutes cold.

**MDE.** None — a proportion over millions of takes, not a test. The
uncertainty is the extrapolation: report a curve over S (5/20/50/100), never
one number.

**Artefact.** Our S may stop a sweeper walking for a target quantity,
overstating the rate; re-run holding their quantity fixed. The 59%
moves with the grouping window, as F-O05's forward window leaked — report 50 ms
/ 250 ms / 1 s. Tape output: what the market did, never what we would have lost.
