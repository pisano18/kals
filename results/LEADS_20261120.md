## Closes the bot never saw (F-C01 x F-I07)

- **F-C01** — a window opens every 15 minutes, 24/7, on 12 crypto series.
- **F-I07** — `discover()` skips an unknown ticker with no error and no log line.

**Measurement.** F-C01 fixes an exact expectation — 96 closes a day per live
series — against which F-I07's silent skip is visible. Per ET day and series,
count distinct close times in Kalshi's settled list against distinct
(series, close) pairs our `universe` records enumerated. The difference is
closes we never looked at: missed money, with nothing today that says so. Unit
is closes, not markets.

**Data.** `fulltape/markets.json` and the bot's `universe` records (`n`, `got`,
`series`, `close_s`). No tape load, no fills.

**Artefact.** `defer_universe` skips refreshes on purpose while a watched market
is inside its window, so a gap can be a deferral — condition on `got` being
present. Four series settle nothing (F-C10): the denominator is not 12. Thursday
~07Z maintenance and `DOWNTIME.json` windows are legitimate; a dead run loses
its log wholesale.

**MDE.** A census: floor is one close, no noise, no power question. A zero gap is
a result — an upper bound on silent loss.

**Cost.** Cheap; an hour with a self-test.

## Seconds into cents (F-I02 x F-M01)

- **F-I02** — the recorded channels include the 1/sec settlement index.
- **F-M01** — fair is `Phi(((locked_sum + tau*spot)/60 - K) / sd)`.

**Measurement.** Spot enters fair and the index channel dates every print, so a
stale print's cost needs no replay and no fills: for each close and tau in
5-45 s, price fair off the print that was current and off the next one, splitting
locked/remaining with `settlewin.partial()`; report the difference in cents by
coin and tau. R18 and LS19 measured the index age we trade at (0.16-0.3 s at
entry); nobody turned seconds into cents. U38 waits weeks of live logging to ask
whether stale-print orders lose more; this says beforehand whether the mechanism
is big enough to bother. Against a +3.9c mean edge (F-P05), a 90th-percentile
one-second move under ~0.4c at tau <= 20 kills U38 for free.

**Data.** `kalshi_data/cfbenchmarks_value` via `load_index`, plus each market's
strike. Index only.

**Artefact.** The index tape is 88.5% present and holes may fall on moving
seconds, biasing the per-second move DOWN — restrict to gap-free pairs, report
both. Sensitivity scales as 1/sd and explodes as tau falls, so a tau-binning
slip reads as a huge effect. Cluster by close time (rho ~0.8), never by market.

**MDE.** Descriptive, no null. The loss-rate version has NO POWER at 12 losing
closes in 248 — live fills only.

**Cost.** Moderate; index an hour at a time, half a day with its self-test.
