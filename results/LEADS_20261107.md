## L1 — were we filled by liquidity that did not exist when we decided?

**The two facts.** F-R05 (the tape's population is "an offer was sitting
there", ours "someone sold it to us" — 31x apart) x F-X07 (order round trip:
median 96 ms, p90 116 ms).

**The measurement.** For every live fill, take `t_ms_decide` and `t_ms_send`
off the order record, add half the round trip as the one-way flight, and find
in `orderbook_delta` when the level we filled on first appeared. Report two
shares, clustered by close time: levels first appearing after `t_ms_decide`,
and levels appearing after the order was already in flight. F-O06 measured
price age at SIGNAL; this measures it at ARRIVAL, and 96 ms is 38% of the
0.25 s age under which every bad fill in the select sample sat.

**Why both.** If much of our supply arrived during the flight, part of F-R05's
31x gap is mechanical, not adverse selection: we are not taking the offer we
priced. Different cause, different fix.

**Data.** Our own live order records (`t_ms_decide`, `t_ms_send`, `latency_ms`,
`tau_at_send`) joined to `orderbook_delta`/`orderbook_snapshot`. No replay, no
pinsim. The fill population is ours, from our logs; the tape supplies only when
a price appeared — what the market did. No loss rate is taken from the tape.

**MDE, before any estimate.** ~283 fills over ~248 closes at ~1.22 independent
per close is ~200-250 effective observations: a proportion near 20% is callable
to about +/-6 pp — "most / some / almost none", not 15% vs 20%.

**What would make it an artefact.**
1. **Clock skew.** Our send times are this box's clock, delta times are
   Kalshi's. A systematic 100 ms offset IS the effect. Bound it first, off
   F-X03 (283/283 fills at or below the signalled price).
2. **Missing deltas.** F-I06 (68,976,084 silently unparsed) and F-I05 (one
   restart, zero lines recovered) each make an old level look new. Re-run
   `tapegaps.py` on the same hours; drop fills inside a silent run.
3. **Unmatchable fills.** F-R05's own 82c XRP fill is not in the replayed book.
   Report the unmatched share FIRST; if large, the result covers only the
   matchable remainder.

**Cost.** Low: ~300 fills, one book reload per affected hour, no new estimator.
Self-test: plant a level appearing mid-flight, require it found; plus an empty
world.
