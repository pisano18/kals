## 1. F-M08 x F-O03 — broken sigma estimator, or informed counterparty?

**Measurement.** On an exogenous tau grid, tag each candidate market-second with its
within-coin volatility quintile (F-M09: never across coins), then measure what the
*market* did: the fraction of takes on the model's side consuming the whole resting
level, and levels swept in the same instant (F-X05). Lowest quintile vs middle, **at
matched resting size**. A counterparty signature in the worst bucket points the fix
at a gate; no difference points it at our sigma estimator.

Needs `orderbook_snapshot`/`delta`, `trade`, `pinlevels_rows.jsonl`: model quantity
against market behaviour, no live fills and no loss rate.

**Artefact if.** (a) Full-level consumption is mechanically likelier on a thin level,
so if low-vol windows carry thinner books the result is depth, not information. (b) Low measured vol may just be a **stale index**: repeated 1/sec
prints collapse sigma. Control is consecutive identical prints per window, from the
raw index alone; if that explains the quintile, stop there. (c) Sampling on trades.

**MDE.** 16,683 rows, design effect 31-46x (F-R02) → ~440 effective closes, ~88 per
quintile: detectable difference ~**14 percentage points** (69% → 83%), smaller means
"no power", not "no effect". Cost medium — book reconstruction via `load_quotes`.

## 2. F-B01 x F-O03 — where is the capacity ceiling, in bank dollars?

**Measurement.** Size follows the bank with no reference to depth; the supply we face
is a level taken whole. Per live signal, read our side's book depth at the signal
millisecond, divide by the size we sent, track the median as the bank grew. Where it
crosses 1.0, more capital buys no more contracts. CURRENT_STATE #9 calls this a
ceiling; this puts a number on it. Needs our signal logs plus the book; fill
*quantity*, not loss rate.

**Artefact if.** (a) Fill size is censored at our own order size, biasing a
fill-based ratio down exactly where we bound it — read depth from the book, never
the fill. (b) Depth at signal is not depth at arrival: our round
trip is 96 ms (F-X07), the first competing take 67 ms (F-O02), so signal-time depth
**overstates** supply. Re-read at +96 ms in the delta stream; report both.

**MDE.** ~360+ orders, 1-2 per close so clustering is mild: resolves the median ratio
to ~±15%, separating 0.5x from 1.0x but not 0.9x. Cost low — ~400 book reads.
