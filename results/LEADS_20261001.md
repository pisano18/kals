# LEADS 2026-10-01

PROPOSALS — nothing here was measured; this session has no market data. Both ask
whether part of the model's 19x overconfidence (F-M04) is mechanical.

## L1 — Is the settlement we compute the one the exchange settled on?

**F-C01** (a window every 15 min on 12 series) x **F-I01** (index 1/sec).

`pincalib.settle_of` builds the settlement from OUR sixty prints;
`fulltape/markets.json` carries Kalshi's own `settle`, the index level. They have
never been compared. Measure, per settled market, `mean(our 60 prints) - settle`
in units of the model's own `sd`. A systematic offset biases every `mu` the live
bot computes, invisibly. Index and `markets.json` only — no replay, no book.

**Artefact if:** `settle` is rounded or in another unit (one quantum of offset is
formatting, not feed error — hard rule 5); the averaging window is
`[close-60, close-1]` one side and `[close-59, close]` the other; or prints are
bucketed by receipt stamp `_rx_ms` not the exchange `time` field. Each mimics a
feed defect exactly.

**n / MDE:** cluster by close TIME — one defect hits twelve coins (F-R01).
Pre-register the bar at ~0.01 sd, below which an offset cannot move a 0.15%
claimed confidence: a null under it is "no effect", a null on under 30 clusters
"no power".

**Cost:** cheap — one pass over the index hours, no `load_quotes`.

## L2 — Are the closes we throw away the interesting ones?

**F-C01** x **F-I10** (the watchdog checks liveness only every 300 s).

`settle_of` returns `None` unless all sixty prints are present, so a holed close
leaves the calibration sample SILENTLY. An outage runs up to five minutes, and
~5% of 57.7M trades land in the last 60 s of a quarter hour (F-I03) — the busiest
seconds are the ones a socket dies in. So F-M04 and the 12.5x -> 28.2x
deterioration (F-M05) may sit on a quiet-biased survivor sample. Measure the
refusal rate, its trend across the tape, and whether refused close-times differ
from accepted ones in volatility and message rate. `tapegaps.py` already splits
"the exchange sent nothing" from "we flushed late".

**Artefact if:** refused closes are characterised from the holed index itself —
that measures the hole, not the market. Use the other channels at those seconds,
and keep receipt-stamp holes with an intact message stamp out of the count:
those are our buffering.

**n / MDE:** the refusal rate is a count, no power needed. The refused-vs-accepted
comparison is bound by the count of refused close-times; under 30 clusters the
answer is "no power", not "no difference".

**Cost:** moderate — `tapegaps.py` exists; the new work joins its presence table
to the close grid and the refusal set.
