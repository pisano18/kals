## 1. F-I10 x F-M08 — is the low-volatility worst arm a collector gap?

- **F-I10** — the watchdog checks liveness every 300 s, so a killed recorder costs up to five minutes of tape.
- **F-M08** — within each coin the lowest measured-volatility fifth is the worst arm (5.19% bad, 43.7x overconfident); the middle fifth is the best (1.38%).

**Measurement.** For every candidate row, count the `cfbenchmarks_value` prints actually present in the window that sets sigma and in the sixty settlement seconds, then cross-tabulate the model's bad rate against the volatility fifth *and* print completeness. Start with the 8 losing closes that carry the arm: if even two sit on incomplete index coverage, the arm is data, not volatility. Count CLOSES, never rows.

**Why it could be real.** A salvaged partial hour (F-I05) has fewer index prints. That depresses a sum-of-squared-moves sigma *and* corrupts `locked_sum`, most of fair value at low tau (F-C06). One cause, both symptoms — the shape of F-R04.

**Artefact.** Read completeness per WINDOW. Per hour-file, a restart reads as a whole missing hour, no partial windows appear, and the check returns a false all-clear. Keep sigma comparisons within coin (F-M09).

**Legal under rule 5:** this is the model's error on the candidate population, not our loss rate.

**Cost.** Minutes for the 8 closes; roughly an hour of compute for the full cross-tab. Do the 8 first.

## 2. F-O01 x F-R02 — does "the other side did not change" have any power?

- **F-O01** — someone else takes the offer within 1 s 87.9% of the time.
- **F-R02** — on the candidate population rho is 0.62-0.69, design effect 31-46x.

**Measurement.** Re-run the early/late contest split (F-O07: 88.6% -> 86.8%) clustered by close TIME, and **state the MDE before the estimate**. If the MDE exceeds 1.8 pp, F-O07 is no-power, not no-effect — and the conclusion it supports, that what moved is the model and not the counterparty, is half a comparison.

**Artefact.** Importing rho 0.62-0.69 from a different statistic. The contest indicator is a per-offer race and its own rho may be far lower; borrowing the bad-rate design effect would manufacture no-power where power exists. Measure rho for the contest indicator itself.

**Cost.** `pincontest --rescore` is seconds; the clustered interval and MDE are an hour of work.
