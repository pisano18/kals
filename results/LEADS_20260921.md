# LEADS 20260921

Three proposals. **Nothing here was measured** — this container holds no data.

## 1. Is the 28.2% zero-fill PROTECTIVE?  (F-P04 x F-P01)

Speed is not the cause, and one loss undoes 27 wins — so a miss is a free
option, and the question is whether the orders that fill nothing are the ones
that would have lost. Split our own 362 live orders on filled / zero-filled
and compare the signed index move from order time to settlement, in units of
the model's own sd. Continuous, because 12 losses carry no rate test.

**Needs:** our live logs, the 1/sec index, settlements. No tape book, no
replay.
**MDE:** clustered by close time, ~248 clusters split ~102/260, so ~0.4 sd.
Finds a gross tilt and nothing subtle; a null is *no power*.
**Artefact if:** zero-fills are enriched in thin books (F-O04), thin books
track volatility, volatility tracks losses — condition on sd. And F-O05:
being taken is partly *caused* by the outcome, so evaluate at the close, a
fixed time, and never split on "was it taken within X".
**Cost:** a log scan joined to the index. Under an hour, no `load_quotes`.

## 2. Is price improvement a gift or a warning?  (F-M02 x F-X03)

76 of 283 live fills came in strictly better than signalled, zero worse. A
resting ask under our limit is either a stale quoter or an informed one. Same
estimator as #1, split on improved / not. Why it matters: at low tau sd has
collapsed, so a small stale price is a large move in sd units.

**Needs:** as #1, plus the signalled price per order.
**MDE:** ~0.4 sd, same clustering.
**Artefact if:** improvement is mechanically the high-edge fills (F-P05) —
condition on edge at signal. Run the random-sign control; if an absolute-move
version fires, the estimator is broken.
**Cost:** the same script, one more column.

## 3. Did we ever size off a stale bank?  (F-B01 x F-C01)

300 s divides 900 s exactly and the re-read is blocked while in position, so a
tick can sit permanently inside that slice. F-B04's geometric shrink after a
loss assumes the re-read lands between the loss and the next entry. Count
closes entered on a bank predating the last settlement, and the size gap in
dollars.

**Needs:** our live logs only.
**MDE:** a count. Zero over 248 closes bounds it under 1.2%.
**Artefact if:** the logs record the attempt rather than the successful
re-read; or restarts (thirteen on 09-19) re-phase the tick too fast for a
lock to persist.
**Cost:** minutes. A verification, not a discovery.
