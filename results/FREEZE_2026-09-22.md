before any post-freeze outcome was read** (one exception and one count-only

## 3. The bars

Every window is "closes after the window start" (close >= start + 60 s).
Every decision bar decides **once, on its first registered n** (section 4).
"Resampled P" = share of 10,000 with-replacement resamples of the units whose
sum is > 0. Nothing here is a backtest: B3/B4 price insurance from OUR OWN
per-second `hedge_quote` records; B1's counterfactual comes from our own
order records and the book they logged.

### B1 -- the 45 s leg at a third vs full vs off (decision)

- **Data:** Kalshi ledger per market (LIVE); `research/earlyhindsight.py`'s
  per-close FULL, FULL-lo and OFF, built from our order records (filled,
  exec_price, limit_sent, leg, tau_at_send, fee_total), their `signal` (size,
  ladder), `sweep_depth`, `hedge` (side, n, price, ladder_n) and start/autosize
  records. barcheck calls `earlyhindsight.freeze_rows()` -- pinned by the
  sha256 of its source (CRLF read as LF) -- and hands it barcheck's OWN ledger
  snapshot.
- **INVALID** (at any n) if the scorer's LIVE column is not Kalshi's within
  max($1, 2%), a 45 s-leg close has no scorer row, a sample close has a
  market whose ledger contract count differs from the logged fills or a log
  fill with no ledger row (the check that caught the 09-22 side bug, which
  had left LIVE exact), or the scorer's code hash is not the registered one.
- **Window:** 11:42:12Z. **Sample:** the first 300 closes.
- **Statistic:** per close d = policy - live ($); mean d; resampled P(sum d > 0);
  largest close's share of the gain. FULL holds a price-through early fill
  (landed >= 2c under what its logged book said) at the count that filled.
  **FULL-lo** is FULL with every market that rests on something the logs never
  held -- a moved early fill, no logged depth, contracts past the logged
  ladder, a hedge scaled up at its average price -- set to the worst of three
  price readings (at the fill, at the landing price, at the decision-time book)
  and zero.
- **PASS** (switch to the passing policy; both -> the larger mean): mean
  >= **+$0.50/close for full** or **>= +$0.25/close for off**, AND resampled P
  >= **0.975**, AND no single close > 50% of the gain -- for full, on FULL AND
  on FULL-lo. **FAIL** otherwise at 300 closes: keep the third.
  **COLLECTING** before. **PENDING** while earlyhindsight is not importable.
- **MDE:** per-WATCHED-close sd of the differences, pre-freeze, from the fixed
  scorer, including the two early-leg closes the bot never summarised (09-17
  21:15 ET -$15.79, 09-19 02:00 ET -$62.28): full - third $3.07 (216 closes
  since the fixes) to $5.61 (467 since the leg began); off - third $1.77 to
  $2.89. At 300 closes, one-sided 2.5%, 80% power: full $0.50-$0.91/close, off
  $0.29-$0.47/close. **Power at the floors is low:** resampling those real
  closes shifted to a true gain equal to the floor, B1 passes FULL 28-54% and
  OFF 36-46% of the time (twice the floor: FULL 61-98%, OFF 96-100%); the
  review's loss-frequency model gives OFF 18%, FULL ~37%. FAIL is the likely
  result whatever the truth: FAIL means "not shown", not "no difference".
- **On PASS:** put the switch to the operator; if he agrees, a versioned change
  after the freeze. **On FAIL:** keep the third; report the ranking once.
- **Blocks:** nothing -- a size, never a gate; hedges size to whatever is held.
- The 0.975 is stricter than the 0.90 first sketched: B1 makes two comparisons
  and sits in a family of four (section 4).

### B2 -- fresh-offer rule on the 45 s leg, out of sample (decision)

- **Data:** `order` records with `leg == "early"`, filled > 0, each paired with
  its `signal` <= 3 s before (`level_age_ms`); Kalshi ledger per market. A
  market is **fresh** if every 45 s fill was on a level < 250 ms old, **older**
  if none was; mixed or missing ages are not classed (counted in the status).
- **Window:** 11:42:12Z. **Sample:** closes in time order until both groups
  hold >= **80** closes.
- **Statistic:** a close is lost if a market in the group lost. One-sided
  Fisher exact test fresh > older. Dollars: minus the fresh markets' ledger
  P&L pro-rated to the 45 s leg's contracts (what refusing them would have
  kept), resampled over closes.
- **PASS:** fresh lose more often AND p <= **0.05** AND kept > $0 with resampled
  P >= **0.90**. **FAIL** at the sample otherwise. **COLLECTING** before.
- **MDE:** ~13-15 fresh and ~15-21 older 45 s closes a day pre-freeze, so the
  sample takes **~6 days (~570 closes), past the freeze**. Power at 80 v 80
  (simulated, 4,000 worlds): 0.69 for C5's in-sample 8.2% v 0.5%; 0.44 for 7%
  v 1%; 0.21 for 5% v 1%. False PASS with nothing real: 0.009. A FAIL is weak
  evidence of no effect.
- **On PASS:** propose a gate refusing 45 s-leg ENTRIES on levels < 250 ms old,
  with its own undo bar -- operator's call, versioned, after the freeze.
  **On FAIL:** C5 closed as in-sample noise; no gate.
- **Blocks (if adopted):** only 45 s-leg entries on levels < 250 ms old. Never a
  <=30 s entry, never a hedge.

### B3 -- post-fill collapse as a hedge trigger, priced (decision)

- **Data:** `order` (exec_price, ask_seen, want, filled, t); `hedge_quote` for
  the same ticker (ask, size, t) -- exists only from v-safety1, so the
  **window opens 11:52:44Z** (at the first hedge_quote); `hedge` records (side,
  price, n); Kalshi ledger per market.
- **Flagged close:** a fill with exec_price <= ask_seen - 2c.
- **Statistic:** loss rate, flagged v other closes (one-sided Fisher). Value per
  flagged close = insurance bought from our own logged quotes, one try a second
  for 30 s from the fill's second, min(held, quoted size) at the quoted ask
  < $1, taker fee included, **minus only the real hedge contracts that covered
  the insured contracts** (each later hedge pro rata to held-at-the-fill /
  position-when-it-fired, in total no more than the insurance bought; a
  top-up bought after the fill was never insured). Implementation rule: the
  belief hedge still runs on whatever the insurance did not cover.
  Resampled over flagged closes.
- **Sample:** settled closes in time order until **20** flagged closes are
  priced.
- **PASS:** flagged lose more AND p <= **0.05** AND value > $0 with resampled
  P >= **0.90** AND no single close > 50% of it. **FAIL** otherwise at the
  sample. **PENDING** without hedge_quote.
- **MDE:** ~2.1-2.2 flagged closes a day, so **~9-10 days**. Replication power
  at 20 v ~400: 0.93 for C6's 26% v 2.3%, 0.61 for 15% v 2.3%; false PASS
  0.019. Value: ~10c insurance on ~78 contracts gives SE ~$7.7/flagged close;
  break-even loss rate ~10%; at 26% the expected value is ~+$12.5/flagged close
  and P >= 0.90 is reached ~64% of the time.
- **On PASS:** propose an immediate hedge on such fills -- operator's call,
  versioned, after the freeze. **On FAIL:** C6 stays a diagnostic.
  **While collecting:** log only; nothing trades on this signal.
- **Blocks:** nothing -- it would ADD a hedge, and must never use the belief
  hedge's retry budget or trip the pintake halt.

### B4 -- the 0.25 hedge trigger, PREREG_hedge.md's bar restored (decision)

Two halves, both PREREG's own words, not new bars:

- **Safety (PREREG 2026-09-12, "the live bar"), on the first 30 real alarms**
  after **2026-09-21T20:12:16Z** (the 0.25 restart): false alarms <= **3%** of
  positions held up to the 30th alarm; mean money back on caught losers
  >= **25c** a hedged contract; a hedge fill on >= **60%** of alarms; and at ANY
  n, no hedge at >= **$1.00**. Data: `hedge_alarm` (ticker, want, t), `hedge`
  (side, price, n, live), fills, ledger.
- **Move bar (PREREG 2026-09-14: >= 10 events, "shows X ahead ... on those
  events alone", "no single event accounts for more than half the
  difference"), on the first 10 events** after the quote log began (11:52:44Z):
  an EVENT is a CLOSE with a held position whose logged belief fell below 60%
  after its first fill (two coins dipping in one close are one event, summed).
  For each trigger 0.25 / 0.30 / 0.60, the insurance legs it would have bought
  at the first second belief fell under it, one try a second for 30 s at the
  logged ask and size. **PASS:** 0.25 ahead of BOTH 0.30 (PREREG's floor) and
  0.60 (what it replaced), each lead with no single event > 50% of it.
  **FAIL** otherwise; a tie is not a lead.
- **A move FAIL carries little information.** When belief crosses both 0.30
  and 0.25, 0.30 fires earlier and cheaper, so it is ahead whether the market
  wins or loses; 0.25 leads only through events that crossed 0.30, never
  reached 0.25, and won -- at 10 events, 1-2 of them, which the single-event
  guard then fails. So on a move FAIL an alternative is put to the operator
  only if it is itself shown ahead of 0.25 under the same guard; otherwise
  the FAIL is reported as weak evidence and nothing is proposed.
- The bar's status is FAIL if either half fails.
- **MDE:** dips below 60% ran ~1.9 a day, so 10 events is ~5 days. 30 real
  alarms at 0.25 is ~3-5 weeks (0.9-1.5 a day). No power statement for the
  move bar: it is a burden-of-proof rule on a setting that skipped its bar.
- **On PASS:** 0.25 stays, now inside PREREG; next safety review at 100 alarms.
  **On safety FAIL:** PREREG says disable, but the operator's standing 09-14
  order is "Keep the hedge" -- it goes to him as a decision. **On move FAIL:**
  put to him 0.30 or 0.60 only if that setting was shown ahead of 0.25 under
  the same guard -- a strategy change, so his call, versioned, after the
  freeze. Raising the trigger hedges more, so no outcome here blocks a hedge.
- **Blocks:** nothing -- a trigger level, never a gate. The hedge path, its
  retries and its bypass of the pintake halt stay as they are.

### B5 -- why the <=30 s window's volume fell 65% (diagnostic)

- **Data:** `order` (filled, leg, tau_at_send); `refused` (gate, close_s, tau,
  budget_left, size_now, and the price each gate writes: `no_offer` writes
  wanted_side + `<side>_ask`/`<side>_ask_size`; the book gates write want +
  price + size, depth_floor also `offered`; the capacity gates, the stale-data
  gates and max_per_market refuse before the book is read and write no
  price). **tau and budget_left
  exist only from v-safety1, so the window opens 11:52:44Z**; refusals are
  logged once per (close, market, gate), so refused volume is an undercount;
  `close_summary` for the close count.
- **Statistic:** per watched close, contracts bought at <= 30 s outside the 45 s
  leg; refused contracts with 3-30 s left, as **two bounds**. LOW: refusals
  where the book was read, our side at 90-98c, counting min(offered, our size)
  -- measured. HIGH: LOW plus every refusal from a gate that fires BEFORE the
  book is read -- capacity gates (close_budget, max_per_close, both_sides,
  attempts, early_once) and stale-data gates (book_stale, book_suspect,
  index_stale, no_sigma) -- at our full size, an upper bound (the truth is 0
  to our size). max_per_market is dropped: it fires only after the market
  already holds its buys (09-22 09:00 ET BTC held 79 = SIZE when it refused).
  Other unpriced refusals and prices outside 90-98c are dropped and counted.
  By gate ("budget used up" when a priced refusal had budget_left <= 0); gap =
  reference - bought. The filter prints how many refusals it read and dropped
  (its own null).
- **Reference:** the higher of map 11's 2,654/day as 27.65 a close (/96) and
  barcheck's own count on era B (09-12..09-19 closes; 25.2 a close measured
  pre-freeze).
- **Answer (PASS), on the first 100 closes, else the first 300 -- only where
  both bounds agree:** (a) bought >= 80% of the reference -> "buying came back
  once the 45 s leg shrank"; else (b) even the HIGH bound of refused volume is
  < 50% of the gap -> "not our gates: the offers were not there at our price";
  else (c) the LOW bound alone covers >= 80% of the gap AND one PRICED gate
  holds >= 50% of the HIGH bound's volume -> that gate. A gate that refuses
  before reading the book is never named: its volume is unknown (the budget
  hypothesis is still answered by (a)). **No answer (FAIL)** at 300: the top
  three causes on the high bound are reported. **COLLECTING** before 100.
- **MDE:** descriptive -- a share of refused volume, not a test.
- **On an answer:** the named cause becomes a written proposal for after the
  freeze with its own pre-registered bar; no gate is loosened on this alone.
- **Blocks:** nothing -- logging only.

## 4. Multiple looks -- controlled, not ignored

- **Within a bar -- a fixed sample.** Each decision bar is computed ONCE, on
  the first N units in time order (B1 300 closes; B2 closes until 80 + 80; B3
  closes until 20 priced flagged; B4 the first 10 events and the first 30
  alarms; B5 the first 100 then 300 closes). Before N it is COLLECTING and its
  running numbers decide nothing (`--brief` shows counts only). After N the
  verdict is fixed; later data is report only. Re-running barcheck hourly
  cannot fish for a PASS -- the self-test plants a null first 300 closes
  followed by 300 bleeding ones and requires FAIL.
- **Across bars -- a 0.10 budget.** Only B1 (two comparisons), B2 and B3 are
  discovery tests. Their false-PASS rates when nothing is real, at the
  registered n: B1-full 0.036, B1-off 0.008 (resampled null worlds from the
  REAL pre-freeze per-close differences -- the first version's normal
  approximation, 0.025 each, understates a lopsided spread; with FULL-lo
  required too, FULL measured 0.021), B2 0.009, B3 0.019 (Fisher exact is
  conservative at these counts; simulated). Sum **0.072 <= 0.10**. Each also
  needs its dollar condition, which can only lower these. An iid close
  resample ignores day-level clustering (09-19 held 68% of the shortfall).
- **Outside the family, and why.** B4 is PREREG's pre-existing bar restored:
  its alternatives are earlier settings, not new ones, and FAIL returns to the
  registered floor, so it cannot "discover" anything; its guard is PREREG's
  single-event rule. B5 names a cause at most and never changes the bot by
  itself. B6 is report only.
- **Settings.** If a bar's `config_keys` change on the live bot, its window
  restarts at the change (above). No pooling across settings.
