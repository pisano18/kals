# LEADS 2026-10-05

**A proposal, not a result** — nothing here was measured, and nothing here
quotes a loss rate for us from the tape.

## 1. Has one close ever committed more than the bound every drawdown number assumes?

F-B07 (the drawdown ladder: 11.6 worst-closes deep at 2.86%, 34.6 at 4.84%)
x F-R04 (every large edge this project produced was a measurement bug).

**Measurement.** F-B07, F-B06 and F-B04 are all chained copies of one exact
per-close bound, `MAX_PER_CLOSE * size * PRICE_CEILING` (F-B03), and that bound
has never been reconciled against what a close actually held. For every close
the live bot traded, rebuild the contracts and cash committed and divide by the
bound in force at that second. Two numbers: the largest realised exposure as a
multiple of the bound, and the largest realised single-close loss from Kalshi's
ledger as a multiple of it. The reason to look now is a known, unmeasured bug —
same-second double sends can build 2-2.7x SIZE (`CURRENT_STATE` open 5,
`--one-coin-max` not in the argv). If it has ever landed, the whole ladder is
understated by that factor and `BANK_BRAKE` is set against the wrong worst case.

**Data.** Kalshi's ledger (`results/kalshi_ledger.json`, `research/pinledger`)
for dollars; the bot's own order and autosize records for size in force and send
times; `research/pinxfer.py` for deposits. No tape, no replay.

**Artefact.** A breach is manufactured, not found, if: a hedged market's two
settled rows are summed instead of netted; `realised` is used instead of
per-market `pnl_c` (it is a running total that resets on restart); the bound is
computed from the argv default rather than the autosize value in force that
second (re-read every 300 s, flat only, F-B01); or a mid-day deposit moved the
bank and so the bound. The ledger must be the spine — the logs miss markets held
when a run died — with the logs supplying only size.

**Power.** State before estimating: a breach is a falsification and needs no n —
one close over the bound kills the chaining. No breach is **not** reassurance; a
rare double-send may simply not have fired in the closes we have, so report it as
`0 of N closes` with its interval and clustered by close time, never as a clean
bill. Say which of the two answered.

**Cost.** Hours. Arithmetic over files already on the box.
