## 1. Quarter-hour slot of the close vs the 3-sigma miss

**F-C01** a window opens every 15 minutes × **F-M11** hour of day does not
predict the miss, 1.13x [0.58, 2.38] against a 2.50x callable lift.

`pinflood.quintiles` ranks on the feature's VALUE, so `hour` tests only a
monotone ramp across the clock number — it could not have found "the :30 slot
is bad". The 15-minute grid gives the right shape: every close lands at :00,
:15, :30 or :45, and those slots are not interchangeable (hourly ladders and data
prints land on the hour and the half hour). Measure the flood rate per slot with the by-close bootstrap, print `mde_lift` and the
multiple-looks threshold FIRST, and report the whole 4-cell table — not a
top-vs-bottom lift, which repeats the shape error.

**Data:** already loaded by `pinflood` — index + settlements. `tm_min` beside
the existing `tm_hour`.

**Artefact:** collector gaps (F-I05 kills whole UTC hours) and Thursday ~07Z
maintenance are not uniform across slots, so a slot could differ by what it is
MISSING. Print per-slot index coverage before the rates.

**Cost:** one feature line, a table, a coverage check — under an hour plus a
`pinflood` run. Likely "no power"; that is a result.

## 2. How stale is the auto-size when a loss lands

**F-B01** size re-reads every 300 s and only while FLAT × **F-B03** worst-case
close loss is `MAX_PER_CLOSE * size * PRICE_CEILING`.

F-B04's comfort — consecutive worst cases shrink 33%, 22%, 15% — assumes size
tracks the bank between losses. F-B01 blocks the re-read whenever anything is open,
and across 12 series the bot is rarely flat, so the pre-loss size may carry
into the next close and the shrinkage never happens. From our own logs: gaps
between consecutive `autosize` records against the 300 s nominal, and per
losing close whether size changed before the next entry. Re-run `pinbank` with
the measured gap.

**Data:** our own live logs (`autosize` carries `t`, `old`, `new`) and the
ledger. Not the tape — our process, not a loss rate.

**Artefact:** `autosize` is written only when size CHANGES, so missing records
are ambiguous between "bank flat" and "never flat"; read the bank alongside to
separate them. Restarts re-initialise size and would hide staleness — count
those periods apart. The gap distribution is a process property (no
close-clustering); only the per-loss question is clustered, at n = 12.

**Cost:** a log-parsing stage plus one `pinbank` re-run. Half a day.
