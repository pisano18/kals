**A PROPOSAL. No market data on this box; nothing below was measured.**


## 1. F-I06 x F-M10 — are the model's per-close numbers computed on imputed settlement windows?

- **F-I06** — Kalshi renamed fields once and 68,976,084 of 68,976,084 deltas
  went unparsed while every stage exited 0.
- **F-M10** — the previous close's miss does not predict the next close's
  3-sigma miss: 0.86x, [0.55, 1.36] over 18,178 coin-closes.

**The code fact that connects them** (read, not measured): `settlewin.partial()`
defaults to `min_frac=0.95` and returns `sum(got) * (want / len(got))` — up to
**3 of the 60 settlement prints may be missing and are silently replaced by the
mean of the ones present**, with no log line and no marker. `endgame.py:333`
calls it with that default, so it is live in the path behind F-M04/M05/M08/M10/M12.

**Measure.** Two numbers. (a) A census: per coin-close, how many
of the 60 settlement-window prints were actually on disk — a histogram. (b) Ground truth: compare our reconstructed 60-print average to
**Kalshi's own published `settle` level** for that market. Closes at 60/60
should agree to the tick; the error on imputed closes is the imputation cost in
index units, measured directly against an independent source.

**Needs.** `cfbenchmarks_value` plus `fulltape/markets.json` for `settle`. No
`load_quotes`, so no 2-3 GB job and no collector contention. Cheap — the
cheapest class of job here; one coin, one week, first.

**MDE, before the estimate.** (a) is a census, so no MDE applies: it either
finds imputed closes or proves there are none. (b) is a paired difference
against ground truth per close; cluster by close time, not by market (F-R01:
12 series ≈ 1.22 independent observations). If the census returns zero imputed
closes, the question is closed permanently and nothing downstream is re-run.

**Artefact if.** (a) The standard reader recovers **zero** lines from a
restart hour, not half (F-I05), so such closes return `None` and are DROPPED,
not imputed — run the census under `gzsalvage` or it undercounts gaps in
exactly the hours that had restarts. (b) Count raw JSON lines, not parsed
ticks: a renamed field path (F-I06) reads as a recording hole, and the two have
different fixes. (c) Census the candidate closes *before* the flood stage's
filtering, or only the imputed ones are visible and the dropped ones are
invisible. (d) `settle` is the index LEVEL, not the outcome — fix units once
from the whole sample (hard rule 5) and use `endgame.outcome_of()`.
