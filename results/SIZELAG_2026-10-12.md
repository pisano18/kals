## Why the lag never bites

- The bot is flat a median **20 s** after a close (p99 185 s, worst 515 s;
  one 900 s give-up at 2026-10-01 07:00Z = Kalshi's Thursday maintenance window,
  no markets open).
- After a LOSING settle the size moves the **same second** (A30 clears
  `autosize_at`): 25 of 25 losing closes that called for a smaller size moved
  at +0 s (3 more were too small to change it; 2 settled after a restart).
- Otherwise a tick is guaranteed by `max(flat, first fill + 300 s)`.
- The next close's first order goes out **>= 670 s** after the bot went flat
  (p1 689 s, median 1,735 s), because the first leg is sent at tau 45, ~855 s
  after the previous close. Timing test: 804 of 804 fresh, 0 at risk.
- Only path to a stale bet: Kalshi takes > ~14 min to finalize a market, so the
  position is still open (tick blocked; give-up is close + 900 s, after the
  close + 855 s early leg). Seen once in a month, during maintenance.
