# LEADS 2026-10-09

PROPOSALS, not results — this session has no data.

## L1 — F-C05 x F-M06: is the variance wrong in SHAPE, not level?

**F-C05** `Var(settle-strike) = 880 sigma^2`. **F-M06** a 1.25x sigma multiplier keeps 44% of
trades and makes the bad rate WORSE, 2.90 -> 3.09%.

A flat multiplier is a monotone transform of `fair` at fixed tau, so it can only re-rank ACROSS
tau: M06's failure fits `sd(tau)` being wrong in SHAPE, which no scalar fixes. Measure
`realised_sd / model_sd` of `(settle - (locked_sum + tau*spot)/60)` on a tau grid
(60/45/30/20/15/10/5 s), by coin-normalised sigma quintile.

- **Data:** 1/sec index + `fulltape` strikes/closes. No replay, no quotes, no `load_quotes`;
  `pincalib.py` is the template.
- **MDE first:** clustered on close time, 12 coins at rho ~0.8 = ~1.22 effective per close, so
  ~1,500 closes gives n_eff ~1,830 per cell, relative se 1.7%: **MDE ~5% in sd** — flat there
  is "no effect", not "no power".
- **Artefact if:** the `spot` print sits inside the locked 60, or lags what the live model saw
  — either fabricates tau-shaped variance, the exact pattern sought. Or coins pooled without
  per-coin sigma normalisation (F-M09), or tau sampled on trade arrivals.
- **Cost:** half a day, one streaming pass, self-test included.

## L2 — F-M03 x F-O06: is resting age just stated confidence wearing a hat?

**F-M03** the model claims 0.15% wrong. **F-O06** prices resting 30 s+ had 0 failures in 302,
post-hoc.

Kill by hand first: 302 fills at a 31-46x design effect (F-R02) is ~7-10 effective
observations, where 2.9% predicts ~0.26 failures — **0 of 302 is no evidence** — and it points
opposite to F-M08: old prices mark quiet markets, whose sigma fifth is the worst. Then
correlate, across the 16,683 `pinlevels_rows.jsonl` candidates and within tau bands, resting age
of the wanted-side ask against STATED confidence and sigma quintile. If age tracks confidence,
O06 is confidence re-labelled and no gate exists.

- **Data:** orderbook snapshot/delta for age, `pinlevels_rows` for confidence, index for
  sigma. **No loss rate for us from the tape.**
- **MDE first:** 16,683 / 31-46 = 360-540 effective, so **MDE |r| ~ 0.14.**
- **Artefact if:** age is dated from our recording's start, not the price's own first
  appearance. A collector restart recovers ZERO lines for that hour (F-I05), so prices after one
  look freshly rested — and those hours are not random.
- **Cost:** one day, mostly the age reconstruction and its gzip guard.
