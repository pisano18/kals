Hot and Cold Closes

The bot bought on 303 of 634 closes over 8 days. The question was whether some hours are reliably busier. They are not: what looks like a hot hour is a calm market, and the clock stops mattering once the market's own movement is held still.

Each row is a fifth of all closes, sorted by how much the price was moving in the five minutes before. The bar is how often the bot found a bet worth taking. The order is perfect: the calmer it is, the more chances appear.

Each split is tested by scrambling the clock labels 2,000 times and asking how often chance alone spreads the squares this far apart. With about 26 closes an hour, only a gap of 38 points or more can be told from luck at all.
