# Idea sweep, 2026-09-25

**Nothing found in this sweep is proven to make money yet.** The biggest possible money is Kalshi's liquidity rewards, where Kalshi pays a daily pot to people who keep orders sitting in the book. Estimates run from about $15 a day to a few hundred, but all of them depend on one thing nobody has seen: Kalshi actually paying this account. One small test settles that for all seven reward ideas at once: at most about $60 at risk, with an answer in 2-3 days. The other survivors are small, about $1-20 a day each, and lumpy (a Tuesday data drop, a hurricane, a surprise White House posting). Most of them are speed races against bots that already exist. Of 63 ideas checked, 33 died, mostly because a faster trader already takes the money or because the good number turned out to be a measurement mistake.

Times below are ET. Full per-idea detail (every checker and refuter write-up, by id): `results/idea_sweep_2026-09-25.json`.

## Found along the way: lose less / identify better

- **pinrun sizes its bets on money it cannot spend.** Kalshi keeps cash in separate pools, and crypto orders can only draw on the crypto pool. pinrun reads the account total ($1,086), but only $947 sits in the crypto pool. So its bets are about 13% bigger than its spendable cash supports: about 91 contracts where 79 would match. An automatic Kalshi setting (15% of cash to the non-crypto pool, 85% to crypto) is switched on; it parked $139 of the 09-19 deposit out of pinrun's reach. No harm has been seen yet. The risk is a close that needs nearly all the cash (stake cap plus hedges, about $940) and finds that $139 missing. The decision is at the end. (N03, I49)
- **The Polymarket recorder misses about 4 in 10 windows.** `scan_poly_ws.py` re-sends slugs it has already subscribed to, and Polymarket then rejects the whole request. Windows starting at :30 and :45 past the hour are never recorded: 22 of 37 listed windows were caught overnight. `research/poly/poly_ws_record.py` line 60 has the same logic. The planned 09-28 read for the Polymarket ideas will have about 60 of every 100 windows unless the recorder is fixed to subscribe only new slugs, or to reconnect each window.
- **The tape has a hole on 09-25.** There are no order-book or ticker records from 3:10 to 3:16 AM ET, and a collector reconnect restarted the sequence numbers around 3:30-4:30 AM ET, which corrupts rebuilt books in that hour. Any study using that hour is contaminated. One idea's "$635 burst" was exactly this.
- **The "same-second double sends" (CURRENT_STATE open item 5) are not a race.** pinrun sends orders one at a time; the doubles are the designed top-ups and scale-ins. The one costly case (BTC 09-23, -$65) came from a scale-in that compares the new price against the average we paid, which our own sweep had pushed up. Two gates added since then already block it. The wording in CURRENT_STATE should change. (I01)
- **pinrun's volatility reading does not lag after big economic releases.** If anything it reads too high. No release-day gate is needed. (I40)
- **Not measured, and it decides a lot: does pinrun's profit grow with its bet size?** Rain, CPI, AI-share, the MLB props maker and NFL ladder ideas were all cut or refuted mainly because their cash would shrink pinrun's bets. If pinrun's extra contracts earn little, that cost is close to zero and several of them come back. This can be measured from the Kalshi ledger by day against pinrun's bet size.
