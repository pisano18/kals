## Coin race

IS IT WORSE: It is making less than its size should, not picking worse. Per dollar bet it earns more than ever (5.3-5.6% at 20/40 against 3.2% at 5 a leg). It has lost 1 small race since 09-24 (-$1.14). It is worse in two real ways. (1) It gets about a fifth to a third as many bets: 14.9 races a day at 5 a leg, 2.9 at 40. The 97-98c offers it lived on have thinned out across the whole market since 09-27, and faster buyers now beat it to about half the cheap offers. (2) The bets left are close races, 6 of 10 of them photo finishes, at 8 times the size. Money per day is about the same as at 5 a leg ($3.49 against $2.89), but one loss now wipes out about 11 days of race profit instead of about 2. The good record at 40 (5 races, 1 tiny loss) is too short to show it is safe at that size. The one loss stayed small by luck.

### Why
- It is NOT picking worse. Per dollar bet it now earns MORE: 5.6% at 20 a leg and 5.3% at 40, against 3.2% at 5 a leg. Since 09-24 it has lost 1 race out of 58: 09-30 at 8:59 PM ET, BTC YES at 90c, -$1.14. The 09-23 tie is the only other bad race since 09-22. Money truth is Kalshi's settlements (main-account ledger), which match the bot's log to the cent except the 09-23 tie, where the old log was wrong and the ledger right.
- What changed is how many bets it gets. Races per day: 14.9 at 5 a leg, 4.6 at 20, 2.9 at 40. So the bet size went up 8 times while money per day barely moved: $2.89/day at 5, $4.34/day at 20, $3.49/day at 40. As a share of what it is allowed to have out, that is 4.8%/day of $60, then 7.2%/day of $60, then 2.9%/day of $120.
- Cause 1: the 97-98c offers it used to live on have mostly gone, across the whole market. At 5 a leg, 7 of every 10 of its bets were at 97-98c. Kalshi's public trade record for all 944 races 09-22..10-01 shows how often ANYONE bought a coin at 97-98c in the last 30 seconds. From 09-22 to 09-26 that was about 35 coins a day (range 16 to 67). Every day since 09-27 it has been about 12 (range 3 to 19). The bot's own 97-98c orders fell from 14.5 a day to 4.2 a day (at 20) and 0.6 a day (at 40). Of the 97-98c chances that did exist, it now acts on about half, down from about 75% on 09-24..09-26. Most of the misses are coins its model rated below the price (13 of 30). Buying at 99c doubled over the same days, so leaders now jump straight past 98c.
- Cause 2: it now loses the race for the cheap offers (90-96c). It still finds about 5-6 a day, the same as before, but half its orders come back empty. Empty orders on cheap offers: 1 of 16 at 5 a leg, 13 of 23 at 20/40. The odds of that being chance are about 1 in 1,000. In Kalshi's trade record, almost every miss had another buyer taking the same offer first, often seconds before us. Examples: 10-01 1:29 PM ET HYPE, about 400 contracts at 89-90c were bought 5.9 s before our order at 92c, which got 0. 09-29 8:59 PM ET XRP, 65 contracts at 95c went within 0.2 s of our order, which got 0. 09-28 8:29 PM ET BTC, 256 contracts at 98c went the same way. Our 5-second 'wait and confirm' rule puts us about 5 s behind buyers who see the same thing.
- Cause 3: the bigger size can't be filled. The bot sends its limit at the single best price it saw, so a 40-contract order only gets what sits at that one price. It filled 39 of every 100 contracts asked at 40, 47 at 20, and 91 at 5. That is an average of 24.7 contracts per race at 40 and 17.8 at 20. On 09-30 at 8:59 PM ET it asked 40 and got 1.26.
- Cause 4: what is left is riskier, and the size makes a loss much bigger. The bets it still gets are cheap ones, and cheap offers only exist when the race is close. So 6 of every 10 races it entered at 20/40 were photo finishes (winner ahead by under 0.75 bp at the close), against 3 of 10 at 5 a leg and only 5-12 of every 100 races across the whole market. Average price paid fell from 96.7c to 93-94c. That is why each dollar earns more (cheap legs since 09-22: +$19.25 on $238, 8.1%; 97-98c legs: +$7.49 on $424, 1.8%). It is also why each bet can lose more.
- What a loss costs now. One full 40-contract leg lost costs about $37 (at 92c) to $39 (at 98c). That is about 11 days of race income at the current $3.49 a day. On 10-01 at 7:14 PM ET it put $73 on two legs of one race (SOL YES and BTC NO, both lose if BTC wins). That race was decided by 0.45 bp and paid $6.36. Losing it would have taken about 21 days to earn back. At 20 a leg a lost leg costs about $19 (about 4.4 days at the 20-a-leg pace). At 5 a leg it was $4.85 (about 1.7 days). The one loss at 40 was small only because just 1.26 contracts were on offer. Filled in full, it would have cost about $36.
- Not a rule or market-format change. The buying rules (last 30 s, 90c floor, 98c ceiling, 5 s confirmation) are unchanged since 09-24. The 09-28 and 09-30 code changes only raised the size limits and added the stop after 2 losses a day. Coin race prices are still whole cents. No other bot of ours trades coin race markets. Not measured: whether the extra bots started 09-28..09-30 slow the race bot's orders at the quarter-hour, because order send times are not logged to the millisecond.

### Fixes
- Size back to 20 a leg (my pick). At 40 it fills only 24.7 contracts a race on average. Capping the 40-period fills at 20 would have made about $2.5 instead of $6.08 over 1.7 days (about $2 a day less). It halves the worst case: about $19 a leg / $37 a race instead of $37 / $73. Revert line: VERSIONS.md v-race20.
- Cap the money on ONE race at about $40, whatever the size per leg. On 10-01 at 7:14 PM ET two legs put $73 on one finish decided by 0.45 bp, to win $6.36. Both legs lose together if the runner-up wins.
- Do NOT go bigger. The limit is how much is on offer, not the size setting. At 40 it filled 39 of every 100 contracts asked.
- Paper-test a shorter wait for cheap offers (2-3 s instead of 5 s, only at 90-96c) before any live change. Evidence: since 09-27, 13 of 23 cheap orders came back empty because other buyers took the same offers, often 1-6 s before our order. The cheap legs are where the money is (8.1% per dollar against 1.8% at 97-98c). Risk: the 5-s rule was set from the replay to dodge bad fills, so a shorter wait could buy more losers. Paper first, with a written pass/fail line in READS_DUE.json.
- Log the race margin (gap_bp) and the model's worth on every live order. Today they are only on the paper records, so no photo-finish skip rule can be checked. A skip using what IS logged (the lead measure under 1.5) would have dodged the one loss (1.47). It would also have skipped 5 winning cheap races (09-25 HYPE 90c, 09-26 ETH/XRP 91c, 09-27 XRP/SOL 90-92c, 09-28 HYPE 91c), which were among the best payers. So no skip rule is supported yet.
- A 97c ceiling (no 98c legs): low priority. Since 09-22 the 97-98c legs made +$7.49 on $424 (136 legs, 0 outright losses, 1 tied race). At 40 a 98c leg risks $39 to make about $0.75, so 1 loss in about 50 legs breaks even. But only 1 such leg happened in the 40 period, so the cap would change almost nothing now. Worth adding only if size stays at 40.
- Small cleanup, no money: 10 of the empty orders since 09-27 were sent after the last-second re-check already showed a higher price, so they could never fill. Skipping those sends wastes nothing and cleans the fill numbers.

**Corrections.**
1. **The 1-contract row is one bad race, not two.** It shows "lost 1, tie 1" (and so does ET day 09-23), but both mean the 09-23 7:15 AM ET race. Kalshi settled it 50c/50c and it cost -$0.95 on $1.95. Since 09-22 there have been only 2 losing races: that tie and the 09-30 8:59 PM ET loss (-$1.14).
2. **"Per dollar bet it earns more than ever … not picking worse" doesn't follow from the data.** The higher return at 20 and 40 a leg comes from paying less (94.2c on average against 96.5c at 5 a leg) and not yet losing a full-size bet. The fair test is losses against what the prices themselves predicted:
   - At 5 a leg the prices predicted about 1.8 losing legs out of 51. It lost 0, so it beat the market's odds.
   - At 20 and 40 a leg the prices predicted 1.0 losing legs out of 18. It lost 1, exactly what the market expected and no better.
   - At 94c a bet needs about 17 wins to cover each loss. It is running at 1 loss in 18 legs.
   - The one loss filled only 1.26 of the 40 contracts it asked for. Filled in full, the 40 period would be about -$29 instead of +$6.08, and the 20 and 40 periods together about -$19 instead of +$16.50.
   - 18 legs can't tell better picking from worse. What can be measured: its bets now lose, by their own prices, about 1 time in 17 (about 6 in 100). At 5 a leg it was about 1 in 29, and the bets are now 4-8 times bigger.
3. **Orders sent after the last-second check already showed a higher price:** 12 since 09-27 (11 since 09-28), not 10.
4. **Cause 3 needs one detail.** The bot sizes its order from everything offered up to 98c but puts the price limit at the cheapest offer. "Asked 40" therefore doesn't mean 40 were on offer at that price. Of 11 orders at 40 a leg:
   - 5 got nothing
   - 2 got a sliver (1.26 of 40, 1 of 14)
   - 4 filled in full

   Most of the shortfall is being beaten to the offer (cause 2). Only some of it is too little on offer at our price.

**Verdict.** "Making less" holds firmly. Races with a bet fell from 14.9 a day at 5 a leg to 2.9 at 40. The bot was up, the weekday comparison still shows the drop, the buying rules haven't changed since 09-22, and the money cap never blocked a bet. Whether it "picks worse" is unknown. It now takes cheaper bets in closer races at 4-8 times the size. At 20 and 40 a leg its record matches what the market's prices predicted, with no edge showing. At 5 a leg it beat them, though that could be luck: about 1 chance in 6. Corrected headline: fewer bets and riskier bets at a bigger size, and the good return per dollar at 20 and 40 a leg rests on one loss that happened to fill only 1.26 contracts.

Coin race, 'why is it doing worse' (from results/pinracepenny-live.jsonl, live records only): per dollar bet it is doing better, not worse. (a) Races it ordered in: 10-37 a day on 9/21-9/26, down to 2-9 a day since 9/27.
(b) Races that even reached the live window (inside 30 s, priced 90c or more): 10-23 a day, down to 3-8 a day. Races the paper copy could fill: 26-60 a day, down to 22-31 a day. So fewer chances are on offer.

(d) Win rate held up: 1 loss in 23 legs since 9/27, on a 1.3-contract leg.
