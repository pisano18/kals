**Proposals, not results** — nothing here was measured. Both are tape-population
questions: what the model computed and what the index did, never our loss rate.

## 1. The gate has an edge FLOOR and no CEILING. Should it?

F-M03 (model claims it is wrong 0.15% of the time) x F-P05 (entry 95.6c, edge
at signal +3.9c).

Bucket the cached rows by `discount_c` (0.3-1, 1-2, 2-5, 5c+) and count how
often the model's side lost, inside one price band and one tau band. The dump
guard already refuses the extreme — 2c offers on the certain side, wrong 77% of
the time — but the shape between 0.3c and there is undrawn. If it is monotone,
a cheap offer is a warning and the gate needs a top.

*Data:* `results/pinlevels_rows.jsonl`, 16,683 rows, gated `live`.

*Artefact:* `discount_c` = fair − price, so edge and price are coupled, and
price already sorts outcomes (live: 6 losses in 106 fills below 96c, 3 in 164
above). A monotone edge curve could be the price curve wearing a hat. Hold
price and tau fixed or it is worthless.

*Power:* cluster by close time. Design effect 31-46x cuts 16,683 rows to ~420
effective; eight cells leaves ~50 each on a 2.9% base, so only a swing of about
+8pp is findable. Print the MDE per cell first — a null is "no power", not "no
effect".

*Cost:* minutes. Re-score, no tape walk.

## 2. Is quote age just the index clock?

F-I01 (index arrives once per second) x F-O06 (every bad fill sat on a price
under 0.25s old; 30s+ prices, 0 failures in 302).

Does quote age still separate outcomes once time-since-index-print is held
fixed? If not, a young quote just means "someone repriced off the 1/sec tick we
also saw", and the gate should read the index move — earlier and free.

*Data:* same rows. **Check coverage first:** `index_age_s` is non-zero on 3,922
of 16,683 rows, `book_age_ms` on only 946. Whether those zeros are real or
unrecorded decides whether this runs at all; read `pinlevels.py` first.

*Artefact:* both ages come off our own receive clock, so shared latency jitter
manufactures the correlation. Needs a shuffle control breaking index-print
alignment while keeping quote age. F-O06 was chosen after seeing the tape and
is not a prior.

*Power:* same clustering wall, split further by conditioning. MDE first.

*Cost:* minutes if the columns are real; a tape walk if not.
