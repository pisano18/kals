## 1. The mechanism (<= 12 lines)

1. Kalshi 15-min crypto binaries (KXBTC15M + 8 live siblings ETH SOL XRP DOGE BNB ZEC HYPE NEAR; ADA/BCH/TON/CRYPTOCOMP list nothing) settle YES iff the mean of the 60 one-second CF Benchmarks prints stamped [close-60, close-1] (the print AT the close is excluded), ROUNDED to the strike's decimals (2 for BTC/ETH/BNB, 4 for SOL/XRP/HYPE/NEAR/ZEC, 7 for DOGE), is >= the strike; strike(N+1) == settle(N) exactly, so there is one strike per window and ladder/basket arbitrage is undefined. Reproduced 25,803/25,803 (CLEANROOM_2026-09-27.md) and 12,607/12,607 (MODEL_CALIBRATION_2026-09-25.md) [index feed]. The true threshold is K - half a rounding unit (pinrun.eff_strike, THEORY.md s11.5, ROUNDING_LEAD_2026-09-27.md); DOGE's displayed floor_strike is cut to 6 decimals -- use custom_strike.floor_strike / the previous settlement value.
2. With tau seconds left, 60-tau prints are locked; the variance of what remains is sigma^2 x sum(k^2, k=1..tau)/3600 (engine.var_factor; THEORY's "880 sigma^2" is a prose typo for the sum-of-squares form, THEORY.md s11.5) so uncertainty collapses far faster than sqrt(tau): locked fraction 0% at 60 s, 25% at 45 s, 50% at 30 s, 83% at 10 s (THEORY.md s1a-ii). Fair = Phi(((locked_sum + tau*spot)/60 - K)/sd); sigma sensitivity is zero at 50c. The edge is buying the near-certain side after the arithmetic is decided but before the price reflects it; it dies where the locked fraction does (buyers of 95-98c lose 2.8/100 at 31-45 s, 3.6 at 46-60 s, 4.9 at 61-90 s [trade tape, by markets], THEORY.md s1a-ii; clean room: 45-30 s window -$558 on train vs 30-0 s +$1,529 [replay], CLEANROOM_2026-09-27.md).
3. The model is wrong only in the "near-certain" tail: above P 0.999 it is 20-80x overconfident at 45/30 s (24 losses in 15,363 cells vs 0.34 claimed), roughly right below 0.995, and 0 losses in ~38,000 cells at 10 s and 5 s [index feed] (MODEL_CALIBRATION_2026-09-25.md). Per coin the index is not a random walk: XRP 1.32x, HYPE 1.69x a random walk's 60-s variance (CLEANROOM_2026-09-27.md) [index feed]. Model error is ~1/40-1/200 of our live loss rate: our losses are adverse fills, not arithmetic (THEORY.md s4, MODEL_CALIBRATION s0.5) [live fills + index feed].
4. Who sells to us at 90-98c in the last 45 s: (a) holders exiting; (b) makers who pay no fee resting offers that are stale; (c) informed sellers -- a level posted <500 ms ago while takers dump our side (PREREG_toxic.md: 87 markets, 8 of 12 early losers, 9.2 losses/100 vs 0.7 for "resting offer, nobody selling") [live fills]; on 2026-09-27 a 1,000-lot flashed at 97.9c and cancelled within 3-15 ms dozens of times a second, and the Kalshi book collapsed 99c -> 55c within 10-20 ms of Coinbase's first down-tick: "the seller WAS the move" (POSTMORTEM_2026-09-27.md) [trade tape + index feed]. Supply, not the bank, caps profit: 51.8% of 5,269 live refusals are no_offer (2,736/2,736 genuinely no ask; 93% had an ask on the losing side = bids on the winner) (THEORY.md s5, s5a) [live fills]; offers under 97.5c at fire time went from 30-68/day (09-14..16) to zero on 9 of 10 days since 09-17 (WHY_NO_MONEY_2026-09-27.md) [live fills].
5. THE POPULATION TRAP: the tape's population is "an offer sat there"; ours is "someone chose to sell to US now". Same gate 2026-09-11: tape 0.11% lose (1/891) vs live 3.4% (2/59), intervals disjoint; the 82c XRP fill we took exists nowhere in the replayed book (THEORY.md s3). Paper beats live by $6-29/day, two identical paper copies drift $18-40 apart in days (PAPERS_1002_1003.md, RECAP_2026-10-01.md). A paper arm can KILL an idea, never DEPLOY one. Clean-room rediscovered it from the other side: 56 of 100 printed contracts gone within the printing second, 70 by the next (CLEANROOM) [trade tape].
6. Second family -- Coin Race (KXCRYPTOLEAD15M: which of BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP/HYPE has the highest return): settles on (close 60-s TWAP)/(open 60-s TWAP), 773/773 reproduced, ranked at full precision (not the 2 decimals the rules say), ties pay 1/n rounded down (RESULTS_coinrace.md, CLEANROOM) [index feed]; the leader is nameable 97.8% at 30 s, 99.5% at 20 s but the market charges accordingly; edge only inside 30 s; "more edge is a danger signal" (a 40c leg flips sign with a 2-s stale forecast) (HANDOFF_ARCHIVE 09-21) [trade tape]. Commodities (KXGOLD15M etc.) settle on ONE Pyth 1-min candle close -> no locked average, U-shaped risk, 9.7x more uncertain at tau 10; killed structurally and lost real money (-$86.74) (THEORY.md s1b, HANDOFF_ARCHIVE 09-11, IDEAS_2026-09-24.md s3 #4) [live fills + trade tape].
7. Fees: takers pay ceil-rounded 0.07 x p x (1-p) per contract (0.33c at 95c, 1.75c at 50c), verified to the cent on 1,268 account fills; makers pay nothing on our series (fee_type quadratic; first non-vacuous maker fill 2026-09-06 is_taker false fee 0) -- but 160 series charge makers 0.0175 x p x (1-p) (0.44c at 50c; earlier maker kills wrongly used 1.75c) (PROJECT_HISTORY.md, HANDOFF_ARCHIVE 6374, IDEA_LEDGER SJ38) [live fills]. Tick: tapered_deci_cent -- 0.1c below 10c and above 90c, 1c between; hourly ladders (KXBTCD) and Coin Race trade whole cents (a 97.8c limit on KXBTCD is rejected invalid_price, v-cent) [live fills].
8. CF Benchmarks: index ids BRTI, ETHUSD_RTI, ... (the WebSocket needs index_ids or it silently delivers nothing; a "subscribed" reply is not success) (RUNBOOK.md). Prints arrive 87 ms median on a live socket, but 13 of 100 arrive >1 s late on the recorder's socket (CLEANROOM); the bot holds a print 0.2 s median after its stamp (FEED_LEAD). CF -> Kalshi 63 ms, Kalshi -> this box 37 ms, our order round trip 100 ms median (RUNBOOK, LATENCY_2026-09-25.md) [live fills]. Exchange books explain 61-78% of the NEXT index print 0-300 ms early but it does not separate our losers (FEED_LEAD_2026-09-24.md) [index feed + live fills].
9. Maintenance: Kalshi is CLOSED every Thursday 03:00-05:00 ET (GET /exchange/schedule; exchange_active false; WS drops; payouts delayed -- a delayed $84.62 payout was once read as a withdrawal) (memory kalshi-closed-thursday, BOTS_REPORT_2026-10-01.md). Earlier notes say "nightly maintenance until ~5 AM ET"; the schedule endpoint says Thursday only.
10. The money shape: a 97c contract that wins 99/100 pays +1.8c after fee; break-even loss rate ~3.4% at 96.5c, ~2.0% at 98c, ~1.5% at 98.5c; our live rate 2.0-2.4 per 100 markets (THEORY.md s2, WHY_NO_MONEY) [live fills]. A loss is 25-50 wins. Losses on different coins cluster 2.4x the independent rate; one close lost all 4 positions on the tape [trade tape]; our own record 1 close in 241 with two losers (CLEANROOM_VS_INCUMBENT s2B) [live fills].
11. Capacity: 15-min books run out past ~3x today's size (median 325 under the ceiling at our fills vs size 78; fill share 72% at size 86, 57% at 250; AUTO_SIZE_MAX 250 at a ~$2,940 bank) (SCALING_PLAN_2026-09-24.md) [live fills]. Hourly BTC books are 6-30x deeper (median 838 vs 28) but trade 1 contract live and fire ~2 closes/day (OPEN_WORK A2, TEST_AUDIT) [live fills]. Impact: buying 125 instead of 10 keeps 10.2-11.3x of 12.5x; slip ~ N^0.65 and is 26x larger at 96c than at 99.8c (HANDOFF_ARCHIVE 2520) [trade tape].
12. What was never shown: that the edge is stable (3 of 4 clean-room designs made 20-40% less on test days; week 2 made $8/day vs $61), that the market is not adapting to us, that the 0.3c edge floor / 98c ceiling / MAX_PER_CLOSE 2 are right numbers (THEORY.md s9, CLEANROOM_VS_INCUMBENT s3) [belief].
