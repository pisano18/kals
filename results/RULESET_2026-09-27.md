## 2. What it costs when it is wrong, per day

- **Toxic gate (live).** When no toxic loss arrives it skips winners worth
  about $8-17 a day (the three days since it was pre-registered: $14, $14-26,
  then the $132 save). A caught loss is worth $50-195. It pays for itself if
  it catches one real loss every 5-10 days; on the whole record it caught 6-9
  in 19 days, 4-6 of them on the one night hedges were broken.
- **H (no hedge inside 5 s).** When it is wrong -- a real crash with 5 s left
  on a market we hold -- it costs the whole position, $60-190 at today's
  size, against $58 saved per false alarm. Such alarms come about once in
  10 days, so a wrong call is roughly -$6 to -$19 a day averaged, a right
  one +$6 a day. On the evidence (index tape 0 of 2 came back; our stream 1
  of 4) the expected sign is negative. It also leaves every position we open
  in the last 5 s uninsurable: 32 legs, $1,987 at risk so far, all won, which
  still allows a true need rate up to 9 in 100.
- **S (second leg off).** About -$1 a day; removes no loss the live rules do
  not already remove.
- **C (10c cap at every second).** About -$7 a day; catches BNB 09-26 by
  0.037c of edge, i.e. by luck.

## 4. What 22 hedges and 20 losses cannot tell apart

- Two rules whose difference is under about $60 cannot be separated: one
  market (XRP 09-19 -$67, BNB 09-26 -$58, BTC 09-23 +$23) moves a total by
  more than most rules' whole net.
- Thirty-six rule variants were scored on the same 20 losing closes. To
  clear chance after that many looks, a rule that touches 5 / 10 / 20 / 40
  / 80 closes must hold 3 / 3 / 4 / 6 / 7 of the losing closes. The best
  observed is chance 5 in 100 (edge cap of 30c above 20 s, one loser); the
  toxic gate is chance 24 in 100 (63 in 100 without 09-19 and 09-27).
- H rests on n = 1 under today's hedge rule; the cutoff (5 s) sits exactly on
  the event; at 8 s it starts costing real saves (-$8.59), at 10 s -$32.40.
- "Only one combination does all three" is true by construction: (c) reduces
  to "undo the BNB hedge", and H is the switch written to undo it after it
  was seen.
- The first half is the only out-of-sample window for the live entry rules,
  and it is negative for them (-$127). The second loser the pre-registered
  bar asked for arrived today and cost $132 to collect; the 10-01 read is
  still the only honest test of the gate.
- "Winners given up" by the gate are real costs, not upper bounds: in the
  paper twin a toxic refusal locked the market out for the close 25 of 39
  times, bought a dearer level 5 of 39 (+$5.73), and 9 never looked again.

## 5. What was NOT measured

- **The BNB 09-26 mechanism itself**: the model at 99.7 in 100 with the
  projected settle 0.24 of a tick from the strike, 5 s left. A rule "refuse
  an entry or an alarm when the projected settle is within one rounding step
  of the strike" was not measured because the bot does not log its locked
  sum. It can be measured on the index feed alone, for every market not just
  ours, without gating a hedge. This is the next measurement.
- **A fill far below the seen ask**: BNB filled at 59.54c against an 89c ask
  seen 90 ms earlier on a 1.07 s-old index; a 30c-better-than-seen fill at
  5 s may itself be the alarm. Not measured.
- **Re-reading the index before a hedge**: the belief sat frozen at 3 in 100
  for the last 4 s while the market's YES ask went 59c, 90c, 57c, 74c, 26c.
  A 1-s confirmation removes nothing under today's rule and would not have
  stopped this hedge (next print 32 in 100); a "re-read the raw index first"
  rule was not measured.
- **Whether profit grows with size.** Both ~$130 losses in four days were
  198 contracts at 97-98c in one market; the loss size was set by size, not
  by the rule. Open ledger item.
- **Hedging at the FIRST sub-0.40 print** (BTC 09-23: 64c other side at
  22 s, we paid 78-87c at 16-17 s; about +$30 IF it filled) -- a tape price,
  a lead only.
- **63 early legs with unknown toxic inputs** (09-11, 09-12, 09-23 tape
  gaps) are treated as not refused; the gate's give-ups may be understated
  there, its removals cannot be.
- **The toxic thresholds' sensitivity** (share > 0.55 keeps 19 winners, +$26,
  still refuses every loser) is fitted on the data that chose them and is not

- Nothing about re-buys after a refusal, and nothing from the tape's loss
