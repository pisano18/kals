## Refuted / not supported
- NEAR was not a model-tail loss: at the honest ~98.3% the bet at 95.5c was
  still positive; the loss came from the fill, not the forecast.
- HYPE was not adversely selected: the market traded 95-99c, mostly 98.9-99.1c, for 4.6 s
  after our 98c fill.
- The v-hedge25 trigger did not delay the HYPE hedge (0.60 fires the same
  second).
- A z-floor or dropping the early leg costs more than it saves (tables above).

## Could not measure
- The cost on winners of a "bid collapsed in the last second" gate built on
  the bot's own order book: that needs `orderbook_delta` replays (230 MB/hour),
  outside this job's memory budget. The trade-tape proxy is above.
- A hedge triggered by the Kalshi price instead of our belief (the market led
  the index print by ~0.9 s in BOTH closes): false-alarm cost not measured.

## Solutions worth testing
1. **Bid-collapse gate (lose less):** refuse an entry when our side's best bid
   fell >= 5c in the last 1 s of the bot's own book. Blocks entries only --
   never a hedge. Validate: log the feature on every signal for ~2 weeks
   (paper arm with the gate, synced to live), score on live fills vs the
   25%-vs-2% split above; pre-register the bar before reading it.
2. **Early leg at the ceiling:** watch, do not ship. Needs ~10 more early
   losses before 2-in-44 means anything.
