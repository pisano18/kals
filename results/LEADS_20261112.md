## 1. F-X03 x F-X07 -- main-leg limit slip: is the next level up free?

- **F-X03** -- a crossing IOC fills at the RESTING order's price, not at our
  limit: 283/283 live fills at or below the signalled price, 76 strictly
  better, ZERO worse.
- **F-X07** -- order round trip from this box is a median 96 ms, p90 116 ms.

**Measurement.** The main leg sends the seen ask as its limit (`--hedge-slip`
is hedge-only, default 0.0). For every empty main-leg order, rebuild the book
at the empty reply and report, as a CURVE over slip
0.1 / 0.2 / 0.5 / 1.0c: what share had a resting ask at or under limit+slip,
how many contracts rested there, and the model's edge remaining at that price.
Because we pay the resting price, a slip costs nothing on orders that already
fill -- it is charged only when the seen level is gone, and above 90c the tick is 0.1c (F-X01), so 0.2c is ~5% of the +3.9c mean
edge at signal.

**Not LS04.** LS04 valued the 277 empties at the SEEN ask; the fix was
co-location (LS24, ~$9/day banked). 22% still fill nothing under 50 ms. This asks whether the book held anything slightly worse, which speed
does not reach.

**Data.** Our own order log (LS04's `zerofill.py` already holds the empty
list) joined to an `orderbook_snapshot` + `orderbook_delta` rebuild. No
replay, no pinsim.

**Artefact.** An ask resting ABOVE our limit is a different population from
the ask we saw -- somebody chose to offer it there, and F-M07 (26x worse where
somebody offered) and F-O06 (every bad fill under 0.25 s old) say that
population may be adversely selected. LS04's 99.3% side-win rate does NOT
transfer, and transferring it is the 31x population error. So this sizes the PRIZE only; the win
rate of a slipped fill exists nowhere but a live arm. Second artefact: had we slipped we would be
taking size a competitor took (F-O02 median 67 ms, F-O03 whole level 68.6%) --
count only depth beyond what the observed competing takes consumed.

**No MDE:** a proportion and a dollar curve, not a test. Pre-register the
$/day bar before looking at it.

**Cost.** Minutes on a warm `flow` book cache, ~100 minutes cold.
