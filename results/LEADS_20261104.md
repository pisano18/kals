## L1 — what killed the level: a faster taker, or the maker's cancel (F-I04 x F-P04)

**F-I04** every trade record carries `taker_outcome_side` — who crossed, which
side, to the millisecond. **F-P04** filled and zero-filled orders had the same
median latency, 96 ms, so the empties were not read as ours to fix.

LS04/LS24 settled that speed pays (empties 24.6% -> 15.5% in Ohio, ~$9/day
banked) and that the dose-response flattens near 15.7% empty at 0-20 ms. Nobody
has asked what that residual IS. For every empty main-leg order, take the window
between our send and Kalshi's reject and ask the tape one question: did a trade
print at that ticker and level with the taker on OUR side? If yes we lost a
race and more speed still buys fills; if no trade and the `orderbook_delta`
shows the level removed, the maker pulled it and further latency spend is dead
money. Report the split, and the same split by index age at send — a cancel
should cluster just after a 1/sec index print, a competing taker should not.

**Data.** Our order log (LS24's `orders.csv` already has send/reject to the ms)
plus the `trade` and `orderbook_delta` channels for those ~900 market-windows.
No replay, no `pinsim`. **Half a day**, one coin-hour at a time — `load_quotes`
holds 2-3 GB and the collector outranks this.

**MDE first.** ~228 laptop-era empties over ~200 closes, clustered at a 31-46x
design effect (F-R02): callable only as a coarse split, so anything inside about
±15 pp of 50/50 is no power, not a mix. It cannot say which one a single empty
was.

**Not a loss rate.** The tape is used only for what the market did (F-R05); the
population is our own orders, and the outcome half is already answered live
(LS24: 275/277 of those sides won).

**Artefact if** our clock and Kalshi's differ by a fraction of the 13-96 ms
window — calibrate on the 283 known fills first (our own prints must be found
and excluded, or we count ourselves as the competitor), and if that offset's
spread exceeds the window the measurement has no resolution and stops there. A
dropped delta stream (F-I06, F-I05) makes every empty look like a cancel:
require a complete delta stream per window and report how many it discards.
