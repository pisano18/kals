## L1 — F-M03 x F-P08: losses clump, but the index says they can't

- **F-M03** — the model claims it will be wrong 0.15% of the time on the live-gate population.
- **F-P08** — the close after one of our losses loses 9.4% (3 of 32) against a 3.2% base; siblings 14.8%; 45-60 min later 0 of 57.

**The tension nothing resolves.** F-P08's lift is ~2.9x. F-M10 looked for that
autocorrelation at the index level and got 0.86x, [0.55, 1.36] over 18,178
coin-closes — 2.9x is **outside** it. So either F-P08 is 3-of-32 noise, or the
mechanism is not the outcome process but the **supply** process: after a flood,
whoever would sell us the wanted side behaves differently.

**The measurement.** Flag a flood from the index alone. In the NEXT close
measure (a) F-O04's "no ask on the wanted side" rate against its 83.3% base and
(b) the `taker_outcome_side` mix (F-I04) — who crosses, into which side.
Continuous rates on a large population, not a rare binary, so it can resolve.
Deliverable: the post-flood change in wanted-side ask availability, with its
interval.

**Data.** Tape only: 1/sec index for the flag, snapshot + delta for
availability, trade channel for the taker mix. Valid tape use.

**MDE before the estimate.** Cluster by close TIME: rho 0.62-0.69, design
effect 31-46x (F-R02), so 12 series are ~1.2 observations. Report effective
flood closes and the smallest detectable shift in 83.3%; below it the answer is
"no power", not "no shift".

**Artefacts that would kill it.**
1. **Regime persistence.** Consecutive closes share a vol regime and F-M12's
   calmest fifth floods 4.28x more. Condition on realised vol per coin per
   window — never pooled (F-M09).
2. **Common factor.** "Last close flooded" is near market-wide at rho ~0.65.
   Compare within close, across coins, where possible.
3. **Our footprint.** We hedge and re-trade after a loss, so our orders are in
   the book. Measure state before our order lands; exclude our ids.
4. **Forward leak.** No window past the decision instant — F-O05 runs 3.18 ->
   88.55 pp as the window widens.
5. **Transport.** A supply rate on the TAPE's population (F-R05, 31x). Never
   our loss rate; no gate without a holdout and a live bar written first.

**Cost.** Half a day, reusing the contest/select machinery. One `load_quotes`
at a time (2-3 GB).
