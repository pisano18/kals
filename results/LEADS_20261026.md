## L1 -- restate break-even from the price we actually pay

**F-P01** Break-even is a **3.58%** close-loss rate. A win pays ~3.7c, a loss
costs ~96c: one loss undoes ~27 wins.
**F-X03** A crossing IOC limit fills at the RESTING order's price, not at our
limit. 283/283 live fills at or below the signalled price; 76 strictly better;
ZERO worse.

**The measurement.** Over every live fill, take (signalled price - realised fill
price) in cents, weight by contracts, and restate the break-even close-loss rate
from realised entries and realised billed fees instead of signalled prices.
Because the win is only ~3.7c, a 0.3c mean improvement moves the bar ~8%
(3.58% -> ~3.87%), and our two live rates -- 2.86% current, 4.84% all history --
**straddle** it, so where it sits decides whether this version is above water.
Two stated facts also fail to reconcile by ~0.4c: a
95.6c mean entry (F-P05) leaves 4.4c gross and ~0.29c of fee, i.e. ~4.1c, not
3.7c. The first output is which of the two is wrong.

**MDE first.** A point estimate, not a difference, so power is not the problem:
at sd(improvement) ~0.4c over ~283 fills clustered by close (~150 effective) the
mean resolves to ~0.09c, about 2.4% of the win. Under 0.1c, this is closed.

**Data.** Our own live order/fill logs (signalled limit, fill price, contracts,
per-order fee) plus `results/kalshi_ledger.json` for money. **No tape, no
replay** -- and the output is a break-even THRESHOLD, never a loss rate.

**What makes it an artefact.** (1) Fees are billed per ORDER and ceilinged
(F-X02) while 28.2% of orders fill nothing (F-P03), so a per-contract fee
inferred from per-order billing misstates the win with the order-size mix.
(2) A hedged market writes TWO settled rows and the hedge recovers ~1/3
(F-P06) -- unhedged wins against hedged losses is a mixed population.
(3) The limit rules changed across versions (v-ceiling978 at 97.8c, v-cap20),
so pooled improvement is partly a log of our own flag changes; split by version.
(4) If mean entry (F-P05) was already computed from fills, the improvement is
already inside 3.58% and the whole result is zero -- check that first.
