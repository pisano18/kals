## L1 — is the model's sd too small, and by how much (F-M02 x F-M09)

- **F-M02** — `sd/sigma` collapses far faster than `sqrt(tau)`.
- **F-M09** — Sigma is not comparable across coins; it scales with the coin's price.

**Measurement.** `engine.var_factor(tau, rho)` takes an autocovariance vector,
and the two callers disagree: `pincalib.py:153,309` passes `[1.0]` (independent
1-second prints) while the live bot passes `IndexState.refresh_autocov`'s lag-1
and lag-2 estimate, **clamped to ±0.45 and truncated at lag 2**. So the
published calibration is not the sd the bot trades on, and the untruncated
profile is unmeasured. Per coin, from the index alone: autocorrelation of
1-second increments out to lag 30, how often the clamp would bind, then
`Var(z)` for `z = (settle - mu)/sd` under three sd's — iid, the bot's clamped
2-lag rho, and the full profile.

**Bar, before the estimate.** To produce the measured overconfidence (claims
0.15% wrong, is 2.90% — F-M03/F-M04) by width alone, sd must be understated
~1.57x, i.e. `Var(z) ~ 2.5`. **Below `Var(z) = 1.5` the width
explanation is dead** and the overconfidence is tail SHAPE (jumps), a different
fix. The same pass answers shape: tail count of `|z| > 2.97` after dividing z
by its own sd.

**MDE.** Width half: ~sqrt(2/n) per coin, a few percent at ~1,500 closes — far
more power than the 1.5 bar needs. Shape half: ~2 expected events per coin at
the 0.15% level, so it must pool and cluster by close TIME (12 coins at rho 0.8
= ~1.22 observations per close, F-R01); it can see 19x, it cannot see 2x.

**Data.** `cfbenchmarks_value` only — no tape, no replay, no fills. Strikes
come from the index itself (`strike(N+1) == settle(N)`, F-C04).

**Artefact if it comes back positive.**
1. `sigma_at` uses a trailing 300 s window that OVERLAPS the settlement window,
   so sigma and the deviation share prints and `Var(z)` is biased DOWN, worse as
   tau grows. Report sigma estimated strictly before the window too.
2. Repeated/stale prints make rho mechanical, not real. Report the
   consecutive-identical-print rate per coin FIRST; if it is high, rho is an
   artefact of the recording.
3. Report rho's sign per coin: negative rho makes sd larger, reversing the
   direction.
4. Bucket on sigma/price, never raw sigma (F-M09), or the buckets sort by coin.

**Cost.** Cheap — one index pass; `pincalib` and `replay.load_index` exist. Half a day.
