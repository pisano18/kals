## L1 — the dump threshold in sd units, not cents (F-C06 x F-I09)

**F-C06** with `tau` left, `60 - tau` settlement prints are locked.
**F-I09** 16,683 candidates, 2,699 refused by the dump guard.

The guard refuses `conf - price > 0.15`, a constant in cents, but the residual
sd collapses as prints lock (F-M02: 9.7x smaller at `tau = 10` than `sqrt(tau)`
implies), so 15c is a different gate at every tau. Compute `discount_c /
sd(tau)` for all 16,683 rows; report refused share within tau bucket. It says
whether one threshold is doing two jobs, and where the guard's unmeasured cost
lies.

**Data.** `results/pinlevels_rows.jsonl`, in the repo. No tape load. **Minutes.**

**MDE first.** Refusals cluster by close at a 31-46x design effect (F-R02), so
gaps under about ±5-8 pp are no power, not no effect.

**Artefact if** `sd` comes from `sqrt(tau)` rather than `pinrun`'s own
`var_factor`/`N_AVG`: that alone manufactures the tau-dependence claimed.

## L2 — what a freshness gate would COST (F-O06 x F-P05)

**F-O06** every bad fill sat on a price under 0.25s old; 30s+ prices had 0
failures in 302, never gated on. **F-P05** mean entry 95.6c, edge +3.9c.

Nobody has measured what gating on F-O06 costs. From **our own fills only**,
reconstruct each price's resting age from the orderbook stream at our fill
timestamps and report the share of our filled contracts, and of our signalled
edge, per age bucket. 90% fresh kills F-O06; 10% fresh makes it cheap to test.

**MDE first — a cost number only, never a loss rate.** ~300 fills at a 31-46x
design effect is ~10 independent closes, so no outcome split is callable and the
tape may not stand in: the candidate rows carrying `book_age_ms` have population
"an offer was sitting there", and their `won` column is the forbidden number
(F-R05).

**Data.** Our fill log plus per-hour orderbook reads for ~300 windows, never the
whole book (2-3 GB; the collector outranks us). **Half a day.**

**Artefact if** a dropped delta makes a price look OLDER than it is (F-I06, 69M
deltas once unparsed; F-I05, a restart loses the hour): "old and clean" becomes
"our feed was broken". Require a complete delta stream for the 60s before each
fill, and report how many fills that discards.
