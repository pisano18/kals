## L1 — Does the `ticker` channel carry the 0.1c grid at all above 90c?

**F-I02** (five channels recorded: `cfbenchmarks_value`, `ticker`, `trade`,
`orderbook_snapshot`, `orderbook_delta`) x **F-X01** (the tick is tapered —
0.1c below 10c and above 90c, 1c between).

Every depth and touch number in the early-tau work is read off `ticker`,
because it is 1.7 MB/hour against 60-80 for the deltas. `earlybook._f` takes
`yes_bid_dollars` when present and otherwise falls back to `yes_bid` divided by
100 — whole cents. Above 90c the real grid is tenths, our mean entry is 95.6c
(F-P05), the depth floor is worth 30% of the money (F-P09), and v-scalein-caps
only scales in on a "genuinely cheaper ask". A fallback hour reports 0.96 where
the truth was 0.957 and nothing errors: 0.96 is a legal price either way. Kalshi
has renamed a field once already and 68,976,084 of 68,976,084 deltas went
unparsed while every stage exited 0 (F-I06).

**The measurement.** Per recorded hour: how many `ticker` rows lack
`yes_bid_dollars` / `yes_ask_dollars` (the fallback fired), and what fraction of
taper-band quotes are exact whole cents in `ticker` versus in
`orderbook_delta`, which parses `price_dollars` at four decimals. Then, in the
same market-second, count the taper-band prices the deltas carry that `ticker`
cannot represent. It is a count, not an estimate — no MDE, and a zero is a
clean null rather than no power.

**Needs.** `kalshi_data/ticker` and `kalshi_data/orderbook_delta`, read through
`gzsalvage`. No replay, no live logs, no settlements.

**Artefact if.** (1) Resting orders genuinely cluster on round cents in the
taper band, so a high whole-cent share is behaviour, not parsing — the
discriminator is `ticker` and the deltas DISAGREEING in one market-second, never
the whole-cent share alone. (2) A truncated gzip member recovers zero lines, not
half (F-I05), so an hour that reads as all-fallback may be salvage: print lines
recovered per hour beside the count. (3) The snapshots carry no level arrays
(`earlyverify2`), so ticker-versus-delta is the only cross-check available —
do not expect a snapshot to arbitrate.

**Cost.** Two channel scans over a sample of hours, one hour at a time to stay
inside the RAM cap. Half a day, no `load_quotes`.

against `engine.tick_at`. This checks whether the recorded INPUT ever had the
