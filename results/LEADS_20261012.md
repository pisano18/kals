# LEADS 2026-10-12

Proposals, not results -- no data here.

## L1. F-C08 x F-M07 -- did the window OPEN lopsided?

- **F-C08** mean `abs(fair - 50c)` at the open is 4.75c; 40% open outside 45-55c.
- **F-M07** the model misses its claim by 0.028pp where nobody offered, 0.725pp where somebody did (26x).

**Measurement.** Add one stratifier to `pinselect.py`'s two-arm comparison
(ask-exists vs no-ask accuracy, same tape and estimator): how far from even
the window OPENED -- sigma-free, as `abs(open_spot - strike)` over that coin's
trailing 15-minute move, never `abs(fair - 50c)`, whose sigma term corrupts
stratifier and call together. If the 0.725pp penalty sits in one half of
the opens, we hold that observable 900 s before the trade.

**Needs** the book hours `pinselect` already walks, `replay.load_index`, and
F-C04's `strike(N+1) == settle(N)`; plus the per-moment row dump it lacks.

**Artefact if** lopsided opens hold their side, putting their candidates at
higher prices and more extreme confidence: then it is price/confidence
stratification, not supply. Compare inside fixed price AND confidence buckets, per
coin (F-M09), clustered by close time (design effect 31-46x). **MDE first
(`mde_pp`, `icc_deff`): above ~0.5pp it cannot split a 0.725pp gap -- stop and
report no power.** Two tape rates differenced, never our loss rate.

**Cost.** One `pinselect`-class walk: hours, OOM-sensitive, alone.

## L2. F-B03 x F-B04 -- does the shrink arrive before the next close?

- **F-B03** worst one close = `MAX_PER_CLOSE * size * PRICE_CEILING`, a bound.
- **F-B04** consecutive worst cases shrink 33%, 22%, 15% of the bank.

**Measurement.** F-B04 assumes size has already fallen when the next close is
bet, but `autosize_tick` runs at most every 300 s and returns early while a
position is open; closes are 15 min apart. From the bot's own records:
after each bank move, how many closes were bet at the pre-move size before
`apply_size` fired? Non-zero means two bad closes cost ~33%+33%, not
33%+22% (a 3.0% day event, F-B06).

**Needs** `results/pinrun-live-*.jsonl` and `pnl_c` from
`results/kalshi_ledger.json`. No tape, no replay.

**Artefact if** the bank move is read off `realised`, which resets on restart
while a hedged market writes two settled rows: use `pnl_c`. And
`classify_bank_move` guesses transfers from balance moves, so drop closes near
an `external` record. Losing closes alone give n = 12 and no power: use every
bank move -- the lag belongs to the sizing loop, not to losses.

**Cost.** Minutes. A log join.
