## L1 — Is F-M05's tripling a change in the candidate FILE, and does it clear its own MDE?

**F-I09** (`pinlevels_rows.jsonl` holds 16,683 candidates, 2,699 of them rows
the live bot REFUSES) x **F-M05** (overconfidence 12.5x -> 28.2x from the first
70% of closes to the last 30%, while stated confidence barely moved,
0.99851 -> 0.99837).

**MDE FIRST, because it may decide the whole thing.** On a 0.15% stated claim
(F-M03) those ratios are ~1.9% -> ~4.2% actual: a 2.4pp move. 16,683 candidates
at the measured 31-46x design effect (F-R02) is only ~360-540 independent
close-equivalents, so the late third holds ~110-160. At a 2.9% base that puts
the early/late MDE near 4pp. **F-M05 may be no-power, not decay** — and it is
currently load-bearing (F-R04: every large effect here has been a bug).

**Measurement.** Two numbers from one file. (1) Restate the ratio in each half
with an interval clustered by close time, so the gap is reported against its own
MDE. (2) Tabulate what the file LOGGED in each half: the dump-guard-refused
share (F-I09's 16.2% overall), the gate version in force, series mix, tau mix,
candidates per close. If the refused share or the gate differ, the halves are
different populations by construction and "the model got worse" is a selection
move. Then recompute the ratio within strata.

**Data.** `pinlevels_rows.jsonl` plus settlements. No book, no replay, no
fills — no `load_quotes`, so no 2-3 GB resident and no collector risk.

**Artefact if:** the ratio's denominator IS stated confidence, so stratifying on
anything that is a function of confidence or edge flattens it mechanically; the
refused share is itself a function of the gate, which changed across the tape,
so composition and decay are collinear and not identified from a time split
alone — find a gate version spanning both halves and split inside it; and a
composition change does not prove the decay is fake, both can be true. Report
which of the two questions the data answered.

**Not ours.** Every number here is the model's error against the index on the
candidate population — never our loss rate.

**Distinct from** the earlier F-M05 angles: 10-01 asked which CLOSES survived
into the sample (quiet bias), 10-04 the >5%-missing-print filter. This asks
which CANDIDATES the file logged, and whether the difference is real at all.
