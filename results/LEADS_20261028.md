## L1 — F-I06 x F-I07: is the only evidence those four series are dead our own blind spot?

**F-I06** a field rename left 68,976,084 of 68,976,084 deltas unparsed while
every stage exited 0. **F-I07** `discover()` skips an unknown ticker with no
error and no log line.

Both say the same thing: **silence here carries no information.** Point that at
F-C10 — `KXADA15M`, `KXBCH15M`, `KXTON15M`, `KXCRYPTOCOMP15M` "return zero
settled markets". That zero is *our* count, from the method F-I07 describes,
and the Coin Race branch was lost that way once. The same false zero has
already cost two weeks: SJ17 was killed on "banks have zero settled markets",
and on 2026-10-16 `/historical` served 4 events and 49-60 markets each.

**The measurement.** For each of the four, settled markets per day over a fixed
7-day window from Kalshi's own history endpoint (read-only GET), against our
`fulltape/markets.json` count for the same tickers and a known-good control
(`KXBTC15M`) over the same window. Then, only if any count is non-zero, book
presence: how many of those closes carried an ask at any price, from
`orderbook_snapshot`. Count one, cheap, answers "does it exist"; count two
decides whether it is worth anything — F-O04 says 83.3% of the time nobody
quotes the side we want, so existence alone buys nothing.

**Data.** One GET per series, `fulltape/markets.json`, `orderbook_snapshot`
hours for the control window. No book reconstruction, no 2-3 GB load.

**Detectability, stated first.** A series serving one settled market a day is
found with certainty in 7 days. What this CANNOT call is profitability: a new
series settles on the same quarter hour as the other twelve at rho ~0.8
(F-R01), so its closes are worth ~1.2 independent observations each, not 13 —
that is a separate and far costlier question. Stop at existence and quoting.

**Artefact if:**
1. the history endpoint is not the path `discover()` walks — SJ17's false zero
   came from exactly that mismatch. Fetch the control through BOTH paths or the
   comparison means nothing;
2. our zero is F-I05 truncation, not a discovery skip — check lines-present
   against rows-parsed for those tickers first, or the fix aims at the wrong bug;
3. a series launched mid-window shows a benign low count. Report its launch date.

**Cost.** Minutes for the API leg. Under an hour for the book leg.
