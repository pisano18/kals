## L1 — busy-ness against the INDEX miss, not against our 23 losers

**F-I02** the `trade` channel is recorded, and every record carries who crossed
(F-I04). **F-M11** hour of day does not predict the model's 3-sigma miss:
1.13x [0.58, 2.38] over 18,178 coin-closes, against a 2.50x smallest-callable
lift.

**The measurement.** LS16 froze a busy gate — `taker_n_3s >= 3x` the coin's own
median — and tested it against *our* settled markets: 1,110 markets, 23 losers,
which is why it is still "lead, not adopted" with a re-read waiting at 11-23.
The flood sample is the same question with ~60x the events: score per-coin
busy-ness against the **index's** 3-sigma miss over all 18,178 coin-closes.
Busy-ness must be taken from a window that CLOSES before the miss window opens
(the first ten minutes of the window, or up to entry tau), never the last 3
seconds. Report it *inside* F-M12's choppiness quintiles, since the calmest
fifth already floods 4.28% against the choppiest 1.05%.

**MDE first.** This design called F-M12's 4.05x; splitting within choppiness
cuts each cell fivefold, so the smallest callable lift is roughly 3-4x. Under
that reads as **no power, not no effect**.

**Data.** `trade` channel plus `cfbenchmarks_value`. Both already loaded by the
flood stage; this adds one covariate. Hours, not a day.

**What would make it an artefact.**
1. *Leakage.* Taker count is caused by the index moving. A window overlapping
   the miss window measures the answer (F-O05's lesson). Kill it if the result
   survives only on the near-close window.
2. *Coin composition.* Medians run BNB 418 to BTC 42,006. A raw count sorts by
   coin, F-M09's trap. Per-coin ratio only.
3. *Choppiness re-labelled.* Busy and choppy likely move together, so a raw
   lift may be F-M12 in new clothes.
4. *Clustering.* 12 series per close at rho 0.62-0.69, design effect 31-46x.
   Counting 18,178 coin-closes as 18,178 observations manufactures the result.

**What it is NOT.** The index-miss population is not our loss population
(F-R05). A confirmed link is a reason to believe LS16's gate — never a figure
for what the gate saves us. That number comes from live fills only.
