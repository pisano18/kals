## L1 — is the counterparty informed only in the calm fifth?

**The two facts.** F-M08 (within each coin, the LOWEST measured-volatility
fifth is the worst: 5.19% bad, 43.7x overconfident; the middle fifth 1.38%,
8.3x) x F-P10 (touch market-making earns +0.48c per fill, t=+6.4, on 17.1M
fills — and the takers there carry ZERO information, t=0.2).

**The measurement.** Re-cut both of F-P10's numbers by the same within-coin
trailing-sigma fifth F-M08 uses: per-fill markout at the touch, and the
taker-informativeness statistic, one cell per fifth. F-P10's t=0.2 is an
average over every volatility regime; F-M08 names the one regime where our
own model breaks. If the takers are informed only in the calmest fifth, the
zero is a mix and the calm-window danger has a second cause — the other side
knows something — not just a bad sd estimate. F-M06 (scaling sigma makes the bad rate worse, 2.90% -> 3.09%)
already rules out "distrust the model more", so cause matters.

**Data.** `trade` (F-I04 gives who crossed, to the millisecond),
`orderbook_snapshot`/`orderbook_delta` for the touch, and
`cfbenchmarks_value` for the sigma cut. No replay, no pinsim. Every quantity
is what the market did and what the index did — valid tape uses. **No loss
rate for us is taken from the tape**, and the output is diagnostic: queue
position is still unmeasured and 15-min maker is decided dead
(`MAKER_SIM_2026-09-24.md` s9).

**What would make it an artefact.** (1) Low trailing sigma selects windows
whose next seconds also moved little, shrinking any markout toward zero by
construction — needs a matched random-time control inside each fifth and a
markout standardised by that fifth's own realised sd, not raw cents.
(2) An index outage both depresses measured sigma and truncates the markout,
so outage hours pile into the calmest bin (the F-I10 x F-M08 artefact) — drop
closes with incomplete 1/sec coverage and print how many. (3) Quintile edges
fit in-sample can be one coin or one week: fit per coin, confirm in both
halves.

**MDE, stated first.** 376 hours is ~1,500 quarter-hour closes, ~1,830
effective at rho 0.8 — not 17.1M. Per fifth that is ~350-400 clusters, so
compute the smallest callable markout gap on ~370 observations before looking
at the estimate.

**Cost.** One stage over the trade and book channels; the sigma series and
the touch join both exist. ~A day, most of it the control and the filter.
