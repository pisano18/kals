## L1 — Does the auto-sizer actually shrink after a loss, or does it lag?

**F-B04** (because size follows the bank, consecutive worst-case losses shrink
geometrically: 33%, 22%, 15% of the starting bank) x **F-P08** (0 of 18 closes
following a loss lost; mean dollars after a loss is HIGHER).

B04's geometric shrink — and B07's drawdown depth, which rests on it — assume
size tracks the bank promptly. B01 says the bank is re-read only every 300 s and
only while flat, and after a losing close we may be holding a hedge through
settlement. So census every post-loss close in our live history: the size in
effect, the size the then-current bank implies, and the lag in seconds between
the loss settling and the first size change. No statistics — an accounting count
over every post-loss close.

**Needs:** our own live logs (per-decision size, bank reads, timestamps) plus
Kalshi's ledger and `pinxfer` for the bank. No tape, no replay, no book load.

**Artefact if:** a bot restart falls between the two closes — it re-reads bank
and HWM independently (v-hwm-reset), so the size step is the restart, not the
sizer; the bank is taken from the bot's own `realised`, a running total that
resets on restart, with a hedged market writing two settled rows (double
counting); or a deposit lands nearby (7 deposits, $584.46) and moves size with
no loss involved. Any of the three fakes a clean response or hides a lag.

**MDE / what this cannot answer.** The census needs none — it is a count. The
*other* question, whether post-loss closes are genuinely safer, has NO POWER and
must not be read out of P08: 0 of 18 has an upper bound near 15% against a 3.58%
break-even, and the loss brake (three losing closes stop the run) truncates the
worst sequences out of that sample by construction. "No effect" is not what P08
says; "no power" is. So the output is what the sizer DID, never what we should
have bet.

**Cost:** cheap — hours, our logs only, nothing that touches `kalshi_data` or
loads quotes.

**Why it is worth it:** the losing branch is the valuable one. If size lags the
bank after a loss, real drawdown is deeper than B07 states and the live risk
numbers are optimistic.
