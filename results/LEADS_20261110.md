## L1 — Our zero-fill rate is half what the contest facts predict

**F-O03** (rivals consume the whole level 68.6% of the time) **x F-P03** (28.2%
of our live orders fill NOTHING). With F-O01 (someone takes the offer within
1 s, 87.9%), the chance nothing is left for an arriving order is
0.879 x 0.686 ~ **60%**. We measure **28.2%** — a factor of two apart, never
reconciled.

**The measurement, in order.** (1) Recompute the contest rate over a **96 ms**
window — our median round trip, F-X07 — not 1 s. (2) Only if a gap survives:
for each zero-filled order (209 of 995 at 09-22), classify from the tape what
removed the offer inside that round trip — **a rival print landed** (beaten),
**no print, level gone** (maker pulled), or **level still there** (our own
price band). Output: the three-way count.

**Why it is priority 1 (lose less).** `LATENCY_2026-09-25` s2 found 7 missed
orders where the offer was withdrawn, not taken — 6 on winners, 1 on a loser at
-$76.94 — and said catching that loser erases 18 days of the 80 ms speed gain.
It left this unmeasured, needing `orderbook_delta`. F-P04 (96 ms median on
filled *and* zero-filled orders) rules out our speed, so "pulled" is the live
hypothesis: some zero-fill may be protection and speed may buy losers.

**Data.** Our `pinrun-live-*.jsonl` for the population; `orderbook_delta`,
`orderbook_snapshot` and `trade` for the cause. n = closes.

**Artefacts, in killing order.** (a) **The window is probably
the whole answer** — F-O05: the gap grows 3.18 → 88.55 pp as 250 ms widens to
60 s, so 87.9%-in-1-s is not comparable to 96 ms. If step 1 lands near 28%
there is no finding, only an annotation naming F-O01/F-O03's window. (b) Order
on exchange `ts_ms`, never our clock. (c) F-I06 — an unparsed delta reads as
"pulled"; count deltas per hour first. (d) F-I05 — a two-member gzip hour reads
as zero lines, so its orders all read "pulled".

**Power.** The split is a count; no MDE. Comparing beaten against pulled
outcomes is **not** powered — 7 known pulled cases, design effect 31-46x
(F-R02), under 10 independent observations. **No dollar figure attaches:** we
did not fill these, so that population is the adversely-selected one the tape
cannot see (F-R05).

**Cost.** Step 1, minutes. Step 2, half a day, windowed per order — not
`load_quotes` (2-3 GB; the collector outranks the job).
