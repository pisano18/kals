## 1. Do the model's mistakes come in a row?  (F-B06 x F-M07)

F-B06 — back-to-back losing closes are a 3.0%/day event — assumes each close is
an independent draw. F-M07 says failures concentrate in one sub-population
(somebody sold us the side we wanted: 0.725pp off its own claim against
0.028pp). Volatility regimes persist across fifteen minutes; that population
plausibly does too. If so, F-B06 understates the pair rate, the
three-losing-close brake (F-B05) fires more often than modelled, and F-B07's
drawdown is too shallow.

**Measure:** from the 1/sec index alone, per close, whether the model's call was
wrong (pincalib's quantity, F-M04's 2.90%). Then the chance it is wrong at close
N+1 given it was wrong at N, against the plain rate. Per series first, pooled
second.

**Needs:** index and settlements only. No book, no replay, no tape — this is
"what the index did", never our loss rate.
**MDE:** ~2400 closes, 12 series worth ~1.22 each, base 2.9% leaves ~84
"previous was wrong" cases. Detects the conditional TRIPLING (2.9% -> ~8%);
1.5x is invisible. Relax the gate to 95% for n.
**Artefact if:** (a) **strike(N+1) == settle(N)** — adjacent closes share data BY
CONSTRUCTION (F-C04), so an error at N moves N+1's strike. Control against
closes two apart, which share nothing, plus a shuffled-order null at the same
tau. This one would fake the whole result. (b) Calling close N bad when ANY of
12 series was wrong turns cross-sectional rho 0.62-0.69 (F-R02) into fake
persistence. (c) If it vanishes under volatility quintile it is F-M08 restated.
**Cost:** a second pass over pincalib's per-close output. Hours, no `load_quotes`.

## 2. F-P08's denominator counts closes that held nothing  (F-P03 x F-P08)

28.2% of orders fill nothing, and a zero-fill close cannot lose — so "0 of 18
closes following a loss lost" is nearer 0 of 13. Already weak: at a 4.84% base
rate, zero losses in 18 is the likeliest single outcome (p 0.41).

**Measure:** recount F-P08 over post-loss closes that actually held contracts.
Report the count, not a rate.
**Needs:** our live logs.
**MDE:** ~13 closes needs 3 losses (23%) to reject the base rate — 3.5x. Expect
F-P08 RETIRED as no-power, not confirmed.
**Artefact if:** a hedged market writes two settled rows, mis-identifying the
post-loss close and double-counting the 18; or post-loss closes cluster in a
low-bank period, shrinking size for an unrelated reason.
**Cost:** minutes. A correction, not a discovery.
