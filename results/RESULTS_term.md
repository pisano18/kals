## How to read this

- `n` is always a number of MARKETS or CLOSE-TIME CLUSTERS, never trades.
- A P&L figure means nothing without its null band; the replay stage prints both.
- Every large edge this project has produced so far was a measurement bug. Treat anything eye-catching as a bug until it survives its own null.
