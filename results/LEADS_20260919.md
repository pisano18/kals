## 1. Can our own stake rail refuse the hedge at the worst moment?

F-B03 (worst close loses exactly `MAX_PER_CLOSE * size * PRICE_CEILING` — a
bound) x F-X04 (there is no "no" side: buying NO at q is a BUY at `1-q`).

F-B03 bounds the LOSS, and it is exact. But F-X04 says the hedge is a second
purchase, not a sale of the position, so the CASH standing on one close reaches
entry + hedge while `size = bank/5.88`, `MAX_RUN_STAKE` and `MAX_TAKE_COUNT`
were all derived from entry alone. `HEDGE_MAX_ASK` is 1.00, so the hedge leg can
cost more than the entry it protects. Two numbers: (a) arithmetic worst-case
cash on one close at BANK_BRAKE 3.0, as a fraction of bank — entry is 32%, a
hedge at 0.90 adds ~29%, and at the old brake 1.5 the pair exceeded 100%;
(b) the maximum cash our live logs actually show committed on one close, against
`MAX_RUN_STAKE`. If the rail or the balance can bind, the insurance fails
exactly when it is needed and the loss reverts to unhedged — F-P06's one third
becomes zero.

*Data:* `pinrun.py` / `pintake.py`, live order and balance logs. No tape.

*Artefact:* if Kalshi offsets opposing positions on one market, the cash I sum
from order prices is not the cash the exchange held, and an apparent breach is
my own accounting. Confirm against a `/portfolio/balance` read straddling a
hedge.

*Power:* (a) is arithmetic and needs no n. (b) is a maximum over the 3 hedge
events we have — enough to falsify, never to reassure. Say which one answered.

*Cost:* under an hour.

## 2. Does the 26x selection gap survive clustering?

F-M07 (model off 0.028pp where nobody offered, 0.725pp where somebody did) x
F-R01 (12 series per close ≈ 1.22 observations).

The 0.697pp gap carries no close-time-clustered interval and no MDE, and it is
load-bearing — it is why "the counterparty is informed" counts as settled.
Recompute it clustering on close time and print the MDE first.

*Data:* `results/pinlevels_rows.jsonl`, gated `live`.

*Artefact:* both arms come from the SAME closes, so this is a within-close
contrast; applying F-R02's marginal 31-46x design effect to it would
over-correct and manufacture a false "no power", killing a real result. Pair
within close, then bootstrap over closes.

*Cost:* minutes. Re-score, no tape walk.
