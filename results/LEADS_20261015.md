## L1 — does the model get WORSE as the offer gets BIGGER?

**F-P09** removing the depth floor costs 30% of the money (+15% fills, -35%
mean size). **F-R02** rho on the candidate population is 0.62-0.69, design
effect 31-46x.

Among candidates where somebody *did* offer, rank the offered size into fifths
WITHIN each coin and compute the model's own 3-sigma miss rate per fifth —
model belief vs what the index actually settled at, never a loss rate of ours.
F-M07 compared offer/no-offer (26x); nobody has compared offer *sizes*. If big
offers are the dangerous ones, the depth floor is buying us bigger adverse
fills, which is the lose-less direction.

**Needs:** `pinlevels_rows.jsonl` (16,683 candidates with depth at decision)
plus `fulltape/markets.json` for outcomes. No book reconstruction.

**Artefact if:** size bins sort by coin instead of by depth (F-M09's trap) —
hence within-coin ranks; depth tracks the hour and the coin's liquidity, so the
hour mix must be reported; the logged rows are already a gated population, so
the depth RANGE is truncated and a flat result may be no power, not no effect.
State the MDE at design effect 31-46x, clustered by close time, before the
estimate.

**Cost:** ~half a day plus its self-test. Read-only.

## L2 — did the model decay, or did the market just get calm?

**F-M03** the model claims 0.15% wrong. **F-O07** the counterparty did not
change across the tape; only the model's accuracy did.

F-M05's drift (12.5x -> 28.2x overconfident, stated confidence flat) is
therefore in our own inputs. F-M08 and F-M12 both say calm windows are the
worst. So: reweight later closes to the earlier within-coin sigma-quintile mix
and test whether the drift survives. If it does not, "the model is decaying"
becomes "the market got calmer", which the index tells us live.

**Needs:** the 1/sec `cfbenchmarks_value` feed alone — the preferred source,
no replay, no book.

**Artefact if:** the 70/30 split was chosen after the drift was seen, so report
a continuous trend over time, not two buckets; quintiles must be within coin;
a collector restart voids a whole hour (F-I05), so the surviving-hour mix must
be printed beside the result. MDE and close-clustering first, as in L1.

**Cost:** ~a day; `pincalib.py` is the pattern to copy.
