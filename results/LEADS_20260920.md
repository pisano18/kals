# LEADS 2026-09-20

**Proposals, not results.** No outcome below is our loss rate — each is what
the model computed or what the market offered, which the tape is valid for.

## 1. Is our side offered MORE often exactly where the model is worst?

F-M08 (within-coin lowest-volatility fifth: 5.19% bad, 43.7x overconfident;
middle 1.38%) x F-P04 (filled and zero-filled orders share a 96 ms median).

Split F-O04's availability (our side unoffered 83.3% of the time) by the
within-coin volatility fifth. If the market most readily sells us the fifth
where the model is 43.7x overconfident, that is the selection channel, and the
fifth is known before we bid. F-P04 rules out speed as the cause.

*Data:* `pinlevels_rows.jsonl` + index feed for sigma. No book walk.

*Artefact:* low sigma pushes fair to an extreme, so price band and tau move with
the fifth — hold both fixed, and bucket within-coin (F-M09), or this is a
price-band effect wearing a volatility label.

*Power:* 16,683 rows, design effect 31-46x (F-R02) → ~400 effective. MDE first;
near a 16.7% base rate expect ~7-8pp. Below that: no power, not no effect.

*Cost:* an hour.

## 2. Is the last-minute book just the earlier book?

F-I03 (5% of 57.7M trades fall in the last 60 s) x F-O04 (where our side is
unoffered, 100% carry an ask on the losing side).

5% is BELOW the 6.7% a uniform rate gives, so the final minute is not unusually
active and those losing-side asks may be untouched orders from tau≈800. One
number: the age of resting asks at tau 60→3. Old means book mechanics, not an
informed quoter refusing us.

## 3. Did the model get worse, or did the ask mix change?

F-M05 (overconfidence 12.5x → 28.2x, stated confidence flat) x F-O06 (bad fills
on asks under 0.25 s old; 0 of 302 at 30 s+) x F-O07.

Decompose the early→late rise into a shift in the fresh/stale ask mix versus a
change within each age bucket. F-O07 checked contest, speed and depth, never
age, so "the counterparty did not change" is untested in F-O06's one dimension.

*Data (2 and 3):* `orderbook_snapshot` + `orderbook_delta`; one shared ask-age
index serves both.

*Artefact:* a missed snapshot or a truncated gzip member (F-I05, F-I06) makes a
re-posted ask look ancient — restrict to hours with a clean snapshot→delta chain
and print what that discards. F-O06's cuts were chosen after seeing this tape:
fit early, test late. Tau confounds age: the last 10 s is both our best window
and plausibly the freshest book.

*Cost:* one full delta walk, cheap after.
