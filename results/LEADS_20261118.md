## Is "calm" partly a hole in the tape? (F-I01 x F-I10)

- **F-I01** — the settlement index arrives once per second.
- **F-I10** — the watchdog checks liveness only every 300 s, so a kill costs up
  to five minutes of unrecoverable tape.

**The measurement.** The 1/sec cadence makes per-window coverage an exact
number: expect 300 index prints in the last five minutes, count what is there.
`acgapchk.py` already states the index tape is **88.5% present**, which is an
aggregate — nobody has asked where the holes land. Two things to compute: (1)
per-window coverage of the last 300 s, and whether a window's realised-volatility
quintile tracks its coverage quintile; (2) re-run the flood lift of F-M12 (calmest
fifth floods 4.28% against the choppiest 1.05%, monotone, 4.05x) restricted to
high-coverage windows. If dropped seconds are congestion-driven, a window that
looks calm may be one where we lost the moving seconds — and the calm fifth is
exactly where the model is worst (F-M08, 5.19% bad, 43.7x overconfident). Then
F-M12 and anything gated on it would be measuring our socket, not the market.

**Data.** `kalshi_data/cfbenchmarks_value` only, via `load_index`. Index and
model outputs — no loss rate, no fill population.

**MDE, stated first.** Part (1) is a census, so no MDE: coverage is a count.
Part (2) clusters by close time — 18,178 coin-closes is ~1,500 independent
units at rho 0.8 (F-R01), giving a smallest callable rank correlation near 0.07.
At 88.5% presence a 300-of-300 window is vanishingly rare, so the restriction
must be a threshold; **check first whether enough high-coverage windows exist.**
If they do not, that is NO POWER and must be reported as no power, not as no
effect.

**What makes a positive result an artefact.** Low coverage may be a
*consequence* of volatility rather than a cause of mis-measured calm — a busy
market congests the socket. Discriminator: a congestion drop comes with a
message-rate spike on `trade`/`orderbook_delta`; a recording failure (F-I10, or
F-I05's whole-file gzip loss) is all-channel silence with no spike, in
300-second blocks, across all twelve series at once. An exchange-side skip is
not ours to fix and leaves no local restart signature.

**Cost.** Cheap. One pass over an already-loaded channel plus one re-run of an
existing stage. `acgapchk.py` is the template, including its self-test shape.
