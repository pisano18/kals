Where the money comes from

Money in an hour is three things multiplied: how often a bet appears, how many contracts we get, and what each dollar returns. Each one is tested separately here against shuffled copies of the same data, so a pattern only counts if chance could not have drawn it.

Chance that the next close brings a bet. It falls steadily the longer the silence runs, so dry spells are real, not bad luck. They are the same thing as factor 1: choppy stretches persist.
