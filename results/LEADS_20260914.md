**A PROPOSAL. Nothing here was measured — this session holds no data.**

## 1. F-P11 x F-X05 — sweep pressure BEFORE the signal as a per-event risk sorter

- **F-P11** — no coin is reliably safer: rank correlation between halves +0.35, fails non-monotonically out of sample.
- **F-X05** — sweeps print per level; 59% of same-instant trade groups are multi-leg, median 8 legs.

**Measurement.** For every candidate row in `pinlevels_rows.jsonl` (gated with
`pincontest.gate(rows, "live")`, so the 2,699 dump-guard refusals are out),
walk the `trade` channel for the **60 seconds ending at the signal instant**
and count multi-leg sweep groups and legs. Split on that backward count and
compare the model's realised bad rate against its stated confidence in each
arm. Coin identity failed as a sorter; this is a per-event one.

**Rule 5:** this is the model's error on the candidate population, not our
loss rate, and must never be quoted as one.

**Backward by construction.** F-O05 says any forward window leaks the outcome
(3.18 pp at 250 ms, 88.55 pp at 60 s). Everything counted here is past at
decision time, so nothing leaks, and if it works it is gateable as-is.

**MDE first.** ~13,984 gated rows over ~1,688 closes at a 2.90% base rate;
F-R02's design effect of 31-46x leaves ~300-450 effective observations. On a
30/70 split that is an MDE near **+5 pp**: 2.9% -> ~7.7% is visible, nothing
smaller. Compute the exact MDE from the realised split before looking at the
estimate. A null here is very likely no-power, not no-effect.

**Artefact.** (1) **Coin mix** — BTC prints far more trades, so a sweep count
sorts by coin; stratify within coin. (2) **Era** — the last 30% of the tape is
~2.5x worse (1.87% -> 4.61%); stratify on close time. (3) **Volatility** —
sweeps cluster with movement, and F-M08 found a volatility ordering running the
*wrong* way; hold the volatility fifth fixed, within coin (F-M09).
**Placebo:** re-draw the 60 s window from a random different close in the same
coin and hour. If the effect survives that, it is coin or era, not sweeps.

**Prior against it.** F-P10 — takers at the touch carry zero information
(t = 0.2 on 17.1M fills). If that generalises, this is dead before it starts.

**Cost.** The re-score is seconds. The trade-channel walk is the expense:
a targeted 60 s read per candidate, ~1-2 hours cold, cacheable after. Run one
coin first and check the placebo before paying for the rest.
