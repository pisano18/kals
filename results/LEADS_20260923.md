## 1. Is a hedge fill worse than the quote it was sent into?  (F-P06 x F-X03)

F-X03 is the only evidence we have that our fills are good — 283/283 at or
below the signalled price, 76 strictly better, ZERO worse. Every one of those
is an ENTRY order: we cross into a resting offer and get the resting price. A
hedge is the opposite trade — sent when the collapse is visible to everyone,
into the side running away from us — and F-X03 has never been checked there.
F-P06's cost curve (34.9% of a loss recovered at 20 contracts, 22.3% at 250)
comes from book depth, not from what we paid. If hedge fills land above the
quote, every hedge number we hold, the -$9.35 lifetime and A76's sizing ladder
included, is understated by that amount.

**Measure:** per hedge order ever placed, realised fill VWAP minus the ask
quoted in the second BEFORE it was sent, against hedge size and against belief
at the trigger. Post-2026-09-22 hedges need no replay — `hedge_quote` is logged
per held second (v-safety1).

**Needs:** our own live logs for the post-09-22 hedges (hours). The ~17 earlier
alarms need an orderbook read at those timestamps; `hedgetune.py` already
rebuilds every real-money alarm, so reuse that rather than a fresh replay.

**MDE:** ~18 hedge orders, each a distinct close (collapse any that share a
close time). Paired, that detects about 0.7 sd — if per-order slippage sd is
~8c, MDE ≈ 5.5c a contract, roughly $4 a hedge. It can find a gross error in
our hedge-cost model; it cannot find a 1c one. That is no power, not no effect.

**Artefact if:** (a) slippage is scored against OUR limit — hedges carry
`--hedge-slip`, so "at or below limit" is definitional and would flatter us to
zero. It must be scored against an independently reconstructed quote.
(b) The quote is taken from the SAME second as the order — that print can be
the collapse that triggered us. Use the prior second and show the answer under
±1 s shifts. (c) Partial fills: a big hedge that clears only the top level has a
cheap VWAP and incomplete protection. Score contracts filled against contracts
wanted (`pinfill.py` already tracks wanted through time).
(d) Our `t_ms_*` clock against the exchange's, at a 96 ms round trip (F-X07).
