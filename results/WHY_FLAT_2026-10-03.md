## Verdict (plain words)

1. **45-second cap at 97.8c:** before it existed, those buys made +$131 on 161 markets and lost -$162 on 2 (net -$31 in ~10 days, about -$3/day; Kalshi's per-market money +$0.10 -- a wash). Since it went live it turned away 88 winners worth at most +$47 and 1 loser of -$29 (net at most -$18 in 7 days). **3 losses in total: too few to tell whether it helps; either way it is worth about $3 a day, not the week's gap.**
2. **Seller check:** turned away 55 winners worth at most +$79 (+$53 counting only contracts actually offered) and 3 losers of -$58. Net between +$4 saved and -$22 given up in 7 days. **3 losers: too few to tell.** Its best case (-$132 on 09-27) happened an hour before it went live, so it has not yet had the chance to save a big one.
3. **Why the week was flat:** wins fell $254 (from +$620 to +$366) on half as many markets (351 to 180); the two rules together turned away at most $126 of winners, so they explain at most half of that drop (the rest is not measured here). Losses rose $122 (from -$246 to -$368), and $165 of this week's came from two markets bought before the new rules took effect.
4. **Losses after both rules were live** (-$192 in 5 pin-bot markets): a reversal no rule could see (NEAR -$87, price under 97.8c, no sellers), an old dumped offer (XRP -$43), a buy with 19 s left where the seller check does not apply (SOL -$41), false-alarm insurance (BTC -$13) and a small reversal (BNB -$8). None of the 9/27 rules targets those shapes.
