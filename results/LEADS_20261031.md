## L1 — Where do our closes actually sit on the choppiness ladder?

**F-M12** — The calmest fifth of the last five minutes floods 4.28% of the time
against the choppiest fifth's 1.05% — monotone across all five, 4.05x.
**F-P02** — Current version: 35 closes, 34W 1L. All live history: 12 losing of
248 closes (4.84%).

**The measurement.** Classify every one of our ~248 live closes into F-M12's five
within-coin choppiness bins (within-coin because F-M09: raw sigma sorts by coin),
computing the ruler over `[entry − 300 s, entry]` — ending at our decision, never
at the close. Then report ONE number: the share of our closes in each bin. This is
an exposure count, not a loss rate, so it needs no losses and has no power problem.

**Why it decides something.** The obvious next move — refuse the calm fifth — is
the P05 family, dead on money (−$17 / −$26, negative both halves, refusing 465 of
853 markets) with a wider ruler. Before anyone rebuilds it narrower, exposure
settles it: if we already live in the calm
bins (likely — low measured volatility feeds the model's own confidence, so the
calm reading *buys* us the trade), the gate refuses the bulk of our book and the
family is dead for good. If exposure is flat across bins, F-M12's 4x has somewhere
to land and is worth watching as losses accrue.

**Data.** The 1/sec index feed plus our own decision logs (entry time, coin). No
replay, no tape loss rates.

**What would make it an artefact.**
1. Computing the ruler over a window that runs past our entry. Most of F-M12's
   five minutes sits before entry, but the last ~60 s do not; include them and the
   split is hindsight, not a gate.
2. Ranking on raw sigma instead of within-coin rank — that sorts by coin (F-M09),
   not by choppiness.
3. Treating the 12 series as 12 observations. Cluster by close time: rho ~0.8,
   ~1.22 independent observations per close.

**Cost.** Hours, not a day. One pass over the index and the decision log.

**Power, if anyone goes on to the loss-rate version.** 12 losing closes gives an
MDE around a 3x concentration. A 4x effect is barely visible; 2x is invisible.
That version is "no power", not "no effect", until there are more losing closes.
