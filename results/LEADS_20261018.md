**A PROPOSAL. This box holds no market data and measured nothing.**

## 1. F-O03 x F-P10 — is the informed taker hiding inside the zero?

- **F-O03** — they consume the whole resting level 68.6% of the time.
- **F-P10** — at the touch, makers earn +0.48c per fill (t=+6.4, 17.1M fills) and
  the takers there carry ZERO information (t=0.2).

**Measure.** Split the touch markout that produced P10's t=0.2 on whether the
take cleared the whole resting level or only part of it, matched on resting
size, and report mean markout per arm at several fixed horizons inside the
window. P10 is an average over 17.1M fills; F-O03 says two thirds of takes are
the aggressive kind, so a zero average is consistent with an informed
full-clear arm cancelling an uninformed partial arm. If the split is real,
"the level was just cleared whole" is a live variable the bot already sees in
the trade feed it reads for `toxic_fresh` (F-I04 gives taker side to the
millisecond). If it is zero both ways, full-level consumption is dead as an
informedness marker — which also kills the premise of LEADS_20260924 #1 and
LEADS_20260928 #1 before either is run.

**Needs.** The `RESULTS_informed` touch sample plus pre-trade resting quantity
per fill. Cheap if that intermediate already carries resting and trade size; a
medium `orderbook_delta` pass if it does not. Tape-valid throughout: this is
the counterparty's markout, never our loss rate.

**MDE, before the estimate.** 17.1M fills are not 17.1M observations — cluster
by close time: ~376 h of tape is ~1,500 closes at ~1.22 effective each (F-R01),
so ~1,800. Against a per-fill markout sd of a few cents that detects roughly
0.1-0.2c a fill, i.e. a difference about a third of P10's own +0.48c. Below
~0.1c the answer is no power, not no effect.

**Artefact if.** (a) A thin level is mechanically easier to clear whole, so a
markout gap may be depth — match on resting size, and on price band, since the
tick is 0.1c outside 10-90c and 1c inside (F-X01), making raw-cent markouts
non-comparable across bands. (b) A sweep prints per level, median 8 legs
(F-X05), so message-level counting scores one taker's decision as eight full
clears — collapse same-instant same-side legs to one order. (c) Any horizon
that reaches the close measures the settlement that caused the take, not the
flow (F-O05) — bound every horizon strictly inside the window.
