Two findings matter more than any new trade. One: Kalshi's maker fee on the 100 series that charge one is a quarter of the taker fee (0.44c a contract at 50c, not 1.75c), so earlier maker ideas were killed with a fee 4x too high (SJ38, the one "viable" row). ## How this was done

12 scouts (sports games; props/parlays/futures; elections/politics; econ/fin/commodities; crypto-other; weather/science; entertainment/mentions/companies; cross-venue; exchange mechanics; calibration bias; anything-anywhere; revisit) produced 179 raw ideas; 4 combiners merged them to 58, dropping 17 repeats of ledger kills. 58 checkers measured each idea from Kalshi's own trade history, hourly and 1-minute candles, live books, our index tape and public feeds (never the replay); 22 refuters re-derived the survivors independently; a critic re-cut 3 kills, flagged 12 weak survivors, named 9 missed families and proposed 7 new ideas, which were checked and (for 2) refuted in a second round. Verdicts: 49 dead, 15 need data, 1 viable rule. Caps: roughly 12,000 signed Kalshi GETs over the run at 0.12 s spacing plus Polymarket, ESPN, NHL, TSE, FEC, DOE, NHC and web fetches; RAM under 200 MB per script; no tape loads except hour-by-hour streaming of the index feed; the collector and crypto_feeds were checked alive after each job (32-35 GB disk free). Failures recorded in the JSON: Pyth Hermes now needs a paid key and this box rejects its certificate; kalshi.com's fee PDF answers 429 (read via a proxy); Kalshi's API has purged markets older than about 9 weeks on the Trump-says, Truth Social and golf series, so no holdouts exist there; one checker hung 25 min on a GET with no timeout; several first passes read the wrong orderbook key or treated a candidate list as exhaustive and were re-run before any number was used. Read-only throughout: no orders, no deposits, no process started or stopped; the relayed "Restart the ladder" line was already done at 3:47 AM ET on 10-03 (commit 09a58b8) before this sweep's checkers ran.
