## 6. WHAT WAS TRIED AND FAILED, OR IS UNRESOLVED

### Early entry (buy before 30s) — looks like a loser

Prices ARE better early (p25: 97.9c at 0–10s vs 91.4c at 50–60s, and 1.7x more
sub-95c chances). But the model is **3x more often wrong** (0.17% error at 30s,
0.52% at 55s).

**The live loss that proved it:** ETH at 55s out, model said **99.97% certain**,
bought NO at 96.5c, settled YES, −$19.35. At that same close the live bot
looked at ETH **30 seconds out and its model said 35%.** It was never 99.97%
sure — it did not yet know what it did not know. **The hedge never fired**,
because the failure happened before there was anything to hedge.

Only 5 of 60 settlement prints exist at 55s. **Before 60s, ZERO exist and the
edge is exactly nothing** — `pinwarn.py` refuses `--taus` ≥ 60 for that reason.

Still worth one test: a much stricter gate (99.99%) might filter the fake
certainty. Not yet run.

### Hedge trigger — the biggest untested win

Every trigger from 0.90 to 0.10 rescues **the SAME 25 real losses**. What
changes is false alarms: 38 at 0.90, 1 at 0.10.

| trigger | worth |
|---|---|
| 0.90 | −0.43c/contract |
| **0.80 (LIVE)** | **in the negative band** |
| 0.10 | **+0.49c/contract** |

One false alarm costs ~28c against a 0.72c winning trade — **one needless hedge
undoes ~38 good trades.** Paper arm running at 0.10.

### Buying both sides at once — impossible, dead on arithmetic

NO ask = 1 − yes_bid, so yes_ask + no_ask = 1 + spread. **Always over a dollar.**
Self-tested at three price levels so nobody re-derives it hopefully.

Across TIME it is possible (buy A at 90c, hedge B at 5c = 95c for a guaranteed
$1.00) but rare: the two prices add to a **median of 139–167c**, only 2–5% land
under 100c. **The hedge is a loss-cutter, not a profit engine.**

### Resting limit orders — UNTESTED, and the most promising idea left

Kalshi allows them; `pinrun` deliberately never rests (IOC, `post_only` forced
False). **679 of 2,215 markets (30.7%) saw the winning side touch 95c or better
at some point in the final minute.** A resting bid would have had a chance on
roughly **one close in three**, and makers pay **no fee**.

Risk: a resting bid gets hit exactly when someone knows it is about to flip.
Paper cannot fully test it (our bid would not be there). Would need a small
real-money trial.

### Hourly markets — still the best untested lead

15 of 71 hourly series settle on the SAME 60-second average. Books ARE quoted
through the close (68–94% of reads carry an ask, vs 56–94% on ours). An earlier
"0 of 612 reads had any ask" was **a bug in the probe's own strike selection**
(it sampled only 99c strikes, which cannot have an ask) and is withdrawn.

Untested: whether anyone sells the WINNING side at a price we would pay.

### Crypto perpetuals — operator says a section exists

I scanned all 60,000 open Kalshi markets for "perp"/"funding" and found ZERO.
**He insists there is one.** He owes the name it is listed under. Our edge would
not transfer anyway — perpetuals never settle, so there is no 60-second average.

### Untested / never got to

- Trading VOLUME in `feed_data`
- Quote age (`level_age_ms`) is LOGGED but deliberately not gated on; four
  self-tests assert nothing branches on it. Needs a pre-registered bar.
- A stricter gate (0.9999) to rescue early entry
- Whether 250-contract orders still get 93c — **our biggest fill ever is 55**

## 7. MISTAKES I MADE TODAY — so they are not repeated

1. **Two live bots ran for 24 minutes.** `restart_bot.ps1` matched processes on
   `CommandLine`, which Windows returns EMPTY to a caller that cannot open the
   process. Kill matched nothing, script started a second bot anyway. Nothing
   traded in the overlap. **Fixed: pid file + refuse-to-start + abort-not-fall-
   through.**
2. **`os.kill(pid, 0)` TERMINATES on Windows.** It is not a liveness test
   there. My new guard killed the paper arm testing it. Fixed with
   `OpenProcess(SYNCHRONIZE)`.
3. **A self-test wrote a fake $1,000,000 bank into the real high-water file**,
   so the next live start saw a 100% drawdown and halted. **Bot was down ~3
   minutes.** Fixed by isolating `HWM_FILE` for the whole self-test.
4. **Sorted order-book deltas instead of applying them in arrival order**,
   which resurrected a cancelled 61c order and produced a fake 39c ask. The
   operator caught the contradiction. **Deltas must be applied in arrival
   order.**
5. **Reconstructed a book from the wrong snapshot, then with a timestamp 20
   minutes off.** Third attempt was right. Always reconcile against the bot's
   own logged view.
6. **The `--watch` dashboard process holds stale code.** It overwrote new
   builds for several minutes. Restart it after editing `pindash.py`.
7. **SIX self-tests asserted against a RUNNING value that a new flag changes**,
   each one making the bot refuse to start. **Always assert against
   `_DEFAULT_*`.**
8. **Quoted 7.69% as a loss rate** from a 4-gate simulation next to real
   numbers. The real rate is 4.15%. Tape numbers rank dials; they never
   forecast money.
9. **A `str.replace` anchor that did not exist failed silently**, so a whole
   self-test block was never inserted. **Assert the anchor.**
