## 2. THE NEXT TASK: buying before 30 seconds

### What is known

`research/pinbefore.py`, `results/RESULTS_pinbefore.md`. Re-measured on 14,261
settled closes and ~730,000 moments, at the bar the live gate uses (99.5%):

| seconds left | moments | model wrong | real risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11-20 | 137,472 | 31 | 0.023% |
| **21-30 (today)** | 131,752 | 28 | **0.021%** |
| **31-45** | **183,936** | 107 | **0.058%** (2.8x) |
| 46-60 | 164,994 | 153 | 0.093% (4.4x) |

Tightening the bar does NOT close the gap (2.9x at 99.9%, 2.3x at 99.99%). The
extra risk is inherent to the horizon.

**But model error explains ~1/200th of our losses.** Live loss rate is 4.66%;
model error at 21-30s is 0.021%. Tripling 0.021% moves the expected loss rate
from 4.66% to ~4.70%. The decision turns on whether ADVERSE SELECTION is worse
at 31-45s -- which the index cannot see. Only a paper arm can.

The prize: 40% more moments, and the book is deepest at 28-30s (median 105 on
offer vs 29 at 23-27s), suggesting depth grows further out.

### What the old wall said, and what to do about it

`pinrun.py` line ~211, `TAU_MAX = 30`, carries a comment that ends *"do NOT
extend past 30 on this evidence."* That evidence was 7,302 moments at a 2% bar.
pinbefore supersedes it. **If tau is extended, that comment must be rewritten
to cite pinbefore, not silently contradicted.** The `--tau-max` flag already
exists; no code change is needed for the paper arm.

### The decision rule -- WRITE IT AS `results/PREREG_tau45.md` BEFORE READING THE ARM

CLAUDE.md: no threshold is deployed without a holdout split AND a pre-registered
live bar written before the number is seen. Proposed:

- **Comparison:** the tau-45 arm against the CONTROL arm (pid 411820, identical
  flags, tau 30), not against live.
- **Minimum n:** 30 settled closes in each arm before anything is read.
- **The population that matters:** fills whose signal `tau` is 31-45. Those are
  the ONLY new trades; everything at tau <= 30 should be identical to control.
  Separate them using the `tau` field on `signal` records.
- **Deploy bar:** the 31-45 population has (a) loss rate no worse than live's
  4.66% + noise, (b) positive net cents per contract, (c) at least 20 closes.
- **Kill bar:** loss rate on the 31-45 population above ~7%, OR net negative
  at 30+ closes. One loss costs 9.3 wins; there is no room to be casual.
- **If deployed:** `restart_bot.ps1` gains `--tau-max 45`, `results/VERSIONS.md`
  gets `v-tau45` with the revert line, and `python research/versioncheck.py`
  must pass. The `TAU_MAX` comment in pinrun.py gets rewritten.

### A variant worth a second arm

Rather than a flat 45, a tau-dependent bar: entry at 31-45s only when the
model is >= 99.9% AND the offer is >= some depth. Cheap to run as a third paper
arm alongside. Not started.

## 7. Mistakes I made today -- so you do not

1. **Compared old fills to today's target.** Autosize moves with the bank.
   Score each fill against the `autosize` value in force AT THAT TIME.
2. **`pnl_c` is the WHOLE TRADE in cents, not per contract.** Multiplying by
   fill size produced "$19,554 profit on a $511 bank." Cross-check any total
   against `realised`, which is a RUNNING TOTAL (difference it, never sum it).
3. **Bucketed days by UTC.** A UTC day cuts at 8pm ET. The operator's "$76.31
   yesterday" was Sep 15 ET; my UTC slice said $91.72. Convert BEFORE grouping.
4. **Called today finished with four hours left.** Check the clock.
5. **Quoted a 403 as an entitlement signal.** An unsigned stranger got the same
   403. Always run the control.
6. **GET-probed a POST-only API** and reported 0 of 39 routes. The control
   (four routes from the vendor's own source) caught it.
7. **Wrote a self-test that spells the word it searches for** -- twice, once
   in the assertion and once in the comment explaining it. Build needles.
8. **Nineteen HMAC attempts against an Ed25519 API.** When every rejection
   reads identically, stop guessing and read the docs.
9. **Said "we're in" when authentication worked** before checking the markets
   existed. They did not.
10. **Called the venue hunt a distraction** when pincap said the book was the
    ceiling, then had to reverse when pinfill showed it was not. The operator
    caught both.

Two traps left in tools that otherwise work:
- **`C:\kals\gem_order.py` still posts to `/v1/order/new`, the SPOT route.**
  That is why the event-contract order returned HTTP 500. The operator found
  the real route, `/v1/prediction-markets/order`, and the file was never
  updated to use it. Fix that before any Gemini order test, and keep the
  refuse-SELL and cost-cap guards.
- **`pinshadow.py` shows 0 fills for every paper arm.** Paper arms do not
  write `order` records with `filled` the way live does. Their closes and
  money are real; the fills column is a display gap, not a dead arm.

Venue-specific gotchas: Gemini `master-` keys want the nonce in SECONDS (and
`"account":"primary"` in every payload), `account-` keys take milliseconds;
signature is SHA-384. Polymarket.us signs `{ts}{METHOD}{path}` with the FIRST
32 BYTES of the secret. `result` in markets.json is `'yes'/'no'` -- use
`endgame.outcome_of()`. The auto-mode classifier blocks ad-hoc scripts that
send orders; route them through a named, self-tested file (`gem_order.py`,
`ordercli.py`) -- that is correct behaviour, not an obstacle.
